## How I Think About AI Systems

I do not treat AI output as final truth. I design workflows where AI helps with extraction, drafting, summarization, and prioritization, while important business or technical decisions remain reviewable by humans.

Good AI systems should be:
- grounded in source material
- structured enough to validate
- connected to the real business process
- safe with private data
- easy for non-technical users to review
- measurable by time saved, quality improved, or risk reduced
