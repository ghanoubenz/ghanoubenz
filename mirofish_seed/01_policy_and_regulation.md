# Seed 01 — Algeria: policy, customs, banking and incentive rules affecting a small B2B plastic-injection molder (as of 2026-10-04)

**Context.** A founder plans one 100–120 t injection press, own molds, small B2B plastic parts, in northern Algeria. This document lists the rules that shape costs, timing and competition. It is a seed document for a multi-agent simulation.

**Evidence tags.** `[VERIFIED date · source]` = instrument or figure seen in search-engine snippets of the named source (the research proxy blocked every Algerian site, so no official text was read in full). `[STRONG SIGNAL]` = official announcement or several press reports, no legal text read. `[WEAK SIGNAL]` = single secondary source, undated or indirect. `[UNKNOWN]` = searched, not found. `[ASSUMPTION]` = reasoning, not evidence; these are only in the last section. ## 1. Automotive localisation and the GPI import cut-off

- On 24 Sep 2026 the Prime Minister set 31 Dec 2026 as the end of imports of automotive plastic parts "that can be manufactured by" the state plant GPI (General Plastic Injection, Tissemsilt). From early 2027 licensed car makers must source those parts locally. [STRONG SIGNAL · Radio Algérie, TSA, Algérie Eco, 24–25 Sep 2026; P1-POL §1]
- No legal text (JORA decree or arrêté) and no list of covered parts has been found. A cahier des charges governing GPI–OEM supply is announced but not published. [UNKNOWN · P1-POL §1]
- The PM asked that the molds for GPI's automotive parts be on site before year-end 2026. [STRONG SIGNAL · Radio Algérie EN / TSA; P1-POL §1]
- GPI belongs to the public holding ACS, sits on the assets of the former Plastic Algeria Components (PAC) in Khemisti, Tissemsilt, and was inaugurated 24 Sep 2026. [STRONG SIGNAL · Dzair Tube, TSA, Ecofin; P1-POL §1] Its reported capacity of "251,000 parts/yr for ~200,000 vehicles" is internally inconsistent as published. [WEAK SIGNAL · P1-POL §1]
- GPI's published scope also lists appliances, fridges, air conditioners, cable-industry components, medical, packaging, household goods and road signs. [VERIFIED 2026-09-24 · Algérie360 "gpi-tissemsilt"; data/signals.csv]
- Stellantis/Fiat Algérie targets >30% local integration in 2026; the cahier des charges requires 40% after five years. [STRONG SIGNAL · Algérie360, AlgeriaInvest; P1-POL §2]
- Stellantis signed five contracts with Algerian subcontractors at Mechanica Algeria, Oran, 10–12 Feb 2026, and works with Italy's Sigit and ENPC/Siplast on local plastic parts. [STRONG SIGNAL · TSA, Motorsactu; P1-POL §2]
- More than 200 Algerian SMEs are said to be able to supply the car industry. [VERIFIED 2024-09-26 · Algérie Eco; P2-OS §7]
- The governing vehicle-manufacturing decree text was not read. [UNKNOWN · P1-POL §2]

## 2. Used machinery

- Executive Decree 24-241 of 22 Jul 2024 (JORA no. 50) is the text in force: used production lines and equipment may be cleared only if ≤5 years from manufacture or commissioning (agricultural ≤7), with a conformity certificate, CNRC registration, and entry into service within 6 months. It repeals DE 20-312 (2020) as amended by DE 21-200 (2021). [VERIFIED 2024-07-22 · JORA 50 PDF on douane.gov.dz; P1-POL §3]
- No 2025–2026 amendment of DE 24-241 was found. [UNKNOWN · P1-POL §3]
- Whether the certificate applies per machine or per "chaîne", and whether used equipment can receive AAPI benefits, is not established. [UNKNOWN · P1-POL §3]

## 4. Banking and foreign-exchange rules on imports

- Bank of Algeria Note 01/DGC/2026 (14 May 2026): every goods import must be domiciled before shipment; a 120% provision (deposit and/or credit-line allocation) must be lodged at domiciliation at least 30 days before shipment; transport documents dated before domiciliation are refused save expressly authorised exceptions. [VERIFIED 2026-05-14 · BoA PDF note-01-DGC-2026; BNP Paribas El Djazaïr client notice; P1-POL §5]
- How the note treats supplier advance payments (e.g. a 30% mold deposit), services and small amounts is not addressed in any snippet. [UNKNOWN · P1-POL §5]
- Operators criticised the note; no relaxation was found up to early Oct 2026. [STRONG SIGNAL · Maghreb Émergent; Cap Algérie 20 May 2026; P1-POL §5]
- BoA Instruction 05-2026 (19 May 2026): for imports for resale as-is, an operator's outstanding unsettled imports may not exceed 100% of its equity. It does not appear to target producers. [STRONG SIGNAL · Algérie Eco 19 May 2026; P1-POL §5]
- BoA requires banks to domiciliate exports within 48 h of a complete file. [STRONG SIGNAL · Algérie Eco 28 Jul 2026; P1-POL §5]
- Règlement 2021-01: goods and service import contracts need domiciliation; service imports pay the domiciliation tax; import payment goes only through the domiciliating bank. [VERIFIED 2021-03-28 · BoA exchange regulation page; F-MOLD §8] The domiciliation tax is 0.5% for goods for resale as-is. [VERIFIED n.d. · CMS Law; F-MOLD §8]
- ABEF announced "two new measures" on service imports and domiciliation; content not retrieved. [WEAK SIGNAL · El Watan headline; P1-POL §5]
- Withholding tax on payments to non-resident service providers (sometimes cited as 30%) was not verified. [UNKNOWN · F-MOLD §8]
- Express shipments: DGD instruction 2723/25 (May 2025) reserves simplified procedures for occasional non-commercial shipments; commercial import is reserved to registered operators whose trade-register activity matches the goods. [VERIFIED 2025-05-24 · ObservAlgérie; DHL Algeria customs page; F-MOLD §5]

## 5. Import programme, prior approval and the cabas channel

- The PPI (semi-annual import forecast on a Ministry of Foreign Trade platform) was introduced 9 Jul 2025, renewed for 2026 on a platform launched 16 Dec 2025 for activity codes 01 (production) and 07, and extended to ANADE micro-enterprises; the H1 2026 deadline (26 Feb) was extended. [STRONG SIGNAL · TSA, Twala, Horizons; P1-POL §6]
- The H2 2026 window reportedly closed 31 Jul 2026. [WEAK SIGNAL · Mumnet blog; P1-POL §6] The PPI's legal basis and the 2027 calendar are not published. [UNKNOWN · P1-POL §6]
- Since April 2022 imports have needed Algex approval that the goods are not sufficiently produced locally; the cereal sector reported shortages of inputs and spare parts as a result. [STRONG SIGNAL · Maghreb Émergent; P3 §3]
- Executive Decree 25-170 of 28 Jun 2025 legalised the cabas (suitcase) trade: individuals may import goods for resale up to 1.8 M DZD per trip, twice a month. (Earlier project notes wrongly tied 25-170 to the PPI.) [STRONG SIGNAL · APA News, Algérie Eco; P3 §3]
- Cabas flows are estimated at USD 2–3 bn/yr and include automotive spare parts; customs later tightened controls at Algiers airport. [STRONG SIGNAL · Algérie Eco 24 May 2025; TSA; P3 §3]

## 6. Investment incentives and subcontracting regime

- Law 22-18 (24 Jul 2022): AAPI registration before investing gives customs-duty exemption and VAT franchise on listed equipment during the realisation phase (3 years "secteurs", 5 years "zones/structurants"), and IBS exemption in the operating phase. [VERIFIED 2022 · ZoomAlgérie, FAOLEX text, MFDGI; F-MACH §5; F-REG]
- DE 22-300 (8 Sep 2022) excludes ~160 activities, including "activités de conditionnement et d'emballage", and non-eligible goods including recoverable packaging, road transport and office/IT equipment. [VERIFIED 2022-09-08 · Radio Algérie, Algérie Eco, AAPI investor guide; P1-POL §7] Whether plastics transformation or plastic packaging manufacture is caught is not established. [UNKNOWN · P1-POL §7]
- A 2020 interministerial arrêté gives approved subcontractors of mechanical, electronic and electrical industries a 5-year exemption from customs duty and VAT on components and raw materials, tied to a contracting principal. [STRONG SIGNAL · Algérie Eco 22 Nov 2020; JORA 2020 no. 67; P1-POL §8] Its 2026 status is not known. [UNKNOWN]
- Law 23-12 (public procurement) grants a 25% preference margin for Algerian-origin products with a CCI origin certificate; its implementing regulation was reported pending. [STRONG SIGNAL · CMS Law; P1-POL §10]
- Minister of Industry statements stress subcontracting to raise integration rates. [STRONG SIGNAL · Radio Algérie, undated; P1-BUY §8]
- Solar 1000 MW tenders require ≥30% national integration. [WEAK SIGNAL · Legal Doctrine summary; P2-OS §6]
- A subsidy of up to 60% on drip-irrigation equipment is reported. [WEAK SIGNAL · Ennahar headline, undated; P2-IF §2]
- In Jan 2026 the Commerce Minister declared self-sufficiency in cosmetics, said imports of some cosmetic/hygiene products had stopped, and named local raw materials/packaging as the next challenge. [VERIFIED 2026-01-21 · Cosmetica North Africa coverage; data/signals.csv]

## 7. Budget laws and taxes

- The draft Finance Law 2027 was examined by government on 30 Sep 2026; only general axes are public, no article on plastics, duties or machinery. [STRONG SIGNAL · Le Matin d'Algérie, Maghreb Émergent, Observ'Algérie; P1-POL §9]
- LF 2026 customs changes were sector-specific (solar water heaters, PV inputs, electrolysers, food); none concerned plastics. [STRONG SIGNAL · Oceanic, pv magazine; P1-POL §9]
- IBS is 19% for goods production; standard VAT 19%, reduced 9%; TAP abolished by LF 2024. [VERIFIED n.d. (seen 2026-10-03) · ZoomAlgérie, Ecovis, KPMG LF 2024; F-REG]
- SARL minimum capital 100,000 DZD; creation takes 21–42 business days and costs about 70,700–130,600 DZD. [VERIFIED 2026 · UpGrowth simulator; F-REG]
- The 2026 IFU ceiling and minimum IBS were not confirmed. [UNKNOWN · F-REG]

## Assumptions (not facts)

- Effective protection for a local molder on finished plastic articles is roughly 25 points of duty (30% vs 5%) plus DAPS where it applies. [ASSUMPTION · P1-POL §4 inference]
- Technical components imported as parts of machines or vehicles are classified in chapters 84/85/87 at 5–15% with no DAPS, so protection for B2B components is weaker than chapter-39 rates suggest. [ASSUMPTION · P1-POL §4]
- The GPI ban will be enforced through OEM cahier-des-charges conditions and PPI refusals rather than a tariff-line ban. [ASSUMPTION · P1-POL §1]
- Small parts (clips, grommets, caps) may fall outside GPI's practical capability while facing the same localisation pressure; GPI might subcontract them. [ASSUMPTION · P1-POL §1]
- A 120 t shop overlaps little with GPI's large exterior/interior parts, which need 500–2,500 t presses. [ASSUMPTION · P1-POL §1]
- Plan 3–4 months from purchase order to machine or imported mold on the floor (PPI + domiciliation ≥30 days + 38–45 days sea). [ASSUMPTION · P1-POL §5]
- A new SARL importing press, molds and resin must file a PPI; missing the window can block a mid-semester import. A PPI window for H1 2027 around Dec 2026–Feb 2027 is likely. [ASSUMPTION · P1-POL §6]
- Banks will pay foreign suppliers only against documents (L/C or documentary collection) unless they agree otherwise in writing. [ASSUMPTION · P1-POL §5]
- The AAPI VAT franchise (19%) is the main cash saving, about USD 4–5k on a USD 25k press; registering under a non-packaging activity code (e.g. 111305) avoids the DE 22-300 risk. [ASSUMPTION · P1-POL §7]
- Banking friction and Instruction 05-2026 make imported finished parts slower and costlier for traders, which strengthens a local-supply pitch. [ASSUMPTION · P1-POL §5]
- A competitor with state backing can lobby for import closure on a part family, as GPI did. [ASSUMPTION · P1-POL §1]
