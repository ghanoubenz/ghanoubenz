# MiroFish simulation requirements (paste one at a time into `simulation_requirement`)

All MiroFish outputs must be labelled **SIMULATED OUTCOME** in the study. Keep runs under 40 rounds, as upstream advises because of token cost.

1. **Adoption.** Simulate how maintenance managers, plant owners and procurement officers at UAE SME food, bottling, packaging and plastics plants respond over 24 months to Line Continuity's compressed-air leak programme, and whether they buy the cross-sold critical-wear parts and automation-continuity services. Which customer types adopt first? Which churn, and why?
2. **Competitor response.** Simulate how compressor OEM dealers, 3D-print bureaus, electronics repair labs, condition-monitoring firms and FM companies react when Line Continuity wins its first 10–25 plant accounts. Who copies, who partners, who undercuts, and how fast?
3. **Technical trust.** Simulate plant engineers' trust in non-OEM, locally reverse-engineered and 3D-printed wear parts on production lines, and in non-OEM HMI migrations. What events build or destroy trust?
4. **Pricing.** Compare three price positions for the leak programme (AED 6k / 12k / 18k per year with a guaranteed-savings clause) and for the parts vault (AED 500 / 1,000 / 1,500 per month). Which position maximises retained accounts at month 24?
5. **Supply and scaling risk.** Simulate how delays in TDRA sensor approval, filament or camera supply, repair-lab partner dependence and 47-day payment terms affect the company as it grows from 5 to 25 to 75 accounts.
