# Algerian B2B Buyers for Small Injection-Molded Plastic Components (2025–2026): Target-Account Research

Method note (read first): Research done 2026-10-03. WebFetch was blocked for most Algerian domains (algerie-eco.com, media.stellantis.com, kompass, agro-iss.com, energical.com), so most findings come from search-engine result snippets of the cited pages. I could not open those pages directly. Treat every company below as **"existence verified via search snippet; contacts not verified"** unless marked otherwise. Article publication dates are given where the URL or snippet showed them. **No phone numbers, emails or named buyers were invented.** The one contact block included (STPM Chiali) comes from a search snippet and should be re-checked on the company site.

Verification legend: **[V]** = named in a 2023–2026 news or official source returned by search; **[V-dir]** = shown only in a directory or e-commerce listing; **[UNVERIFIED]** = named in the brief or seen in passing, but I found no confirming source.

## Q1. Electrical installation: manufacturers, distributors, wholesalers, panel builders

### Takeaway
Cable makers (ENICAB Biskra) and transformer and motor makers (Electro-Industries Azazga, now moving fully under Sonelgaz) are confirmed. Local switch and socket makers are hard to verify. Two Algerian brands (Energical, Ghardaïa; Mono Electric) appear online, and Legrand runs an Algerian subsidiary. I found no sourced list of tableautiers (panel builders) or major electrical wholesalers.

### Cited Findings
- **ENICAB (Biskra)** [V]: Algeria's main cable maker. It is a mixed public-private company with about 20,000 t/yr of copper-cable capacity, covering LV, MV and HV cables plus rail, solar and fiber-optic cables. It produces 2,000 t/yr of railway cables and planned 2025 exports to Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Mauritania and France. A buyer of plastic cable accessories (reels, end caps, glands) is plausible but unconfirmed. — [Dzair Tube, 13 Jun 2025](https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/enicab-boosts-rail-sector-with-2000-tons-of-annual-cable-production-eyes-regional-export-expansion); [Algérie Eco: "Câbles pour le ferroviaire : l'Enicab produit 2000 tonnes par an"](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=219513)
- ENICAB signed a convention with CTC Global (USA) in Dec 2022 to produce energy-efficient cables. — [Algérie Eco](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=162908)
- **Electro-Industries / ENEL (Azazga, Tizi Ouzou)** [V]: makes distribution transformers and electric motors, about 3,500 transformers and 13,500 motors per year (capacity 6,000 and 50,000). Sonelgaz becomes 100% owner from Q1 2026. A high-voltage transformer JV with Vijay Electric (India) is due Q1 2027. Plastic needs would be terminal boxes, fan covers and insulators. — [Algérie Eco: "L'ENEL Azazga acquise par Sonelgaz"](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=231616); [Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/75123); [Algérie Eco, Aug 2023, exports to Mauritania](https://www.algerie-eco.com/2023/08/27/tizi-ouzou-electro-industries-exporte-ses-produits-vers-la-mauritanie/)
- **Sitel** [V]: listed as an electrical-cable supplier to the Fiat Oran plant (see Q5). — [Algérie Eco, 20 Oct 2023](https://algerie-eco.com/2023/10/20/usine-fiat-doran-11-equipementiers-deja-selectionnes/)
- **Energical (Ghardaïa)** [V-dir]: Algerian company created in 2006 that sells switches and sockets, including a "DIAA" range, through e-commerce. I could not confirm whether it manufactures or only distributes. — [energical.com category page](https://energical.com/en/product-category/interrupteurs-et-prises/)
- **Mono Electric** [V-dir]: listed on the made-in-algeria.com showcase with switch and socket ranges ("Evolution"). Its current activity is unverified. — [made-in-algeria.com](http://www.made-in-algeria.com/vitrine/3_catalogue.php?tc1=lKyRmaWU)
- **Legrand Algérie** [V-dir]: subsidiary with a local catalogue. It would buy as a multinational, under group procurement. — [legrand.dz](https://www.legrand.dz/fr)
- Online electrical distributors and retailers [V-dir], possible channels for private-label accessories: **SOGEDIM** ([sogedim.net](https://sogedim.net/categorie-produit/electricite-et-eclairage/installations/interrupteurs-et-prises/)) and **Elecmarket-dz** ([elecmarket-dz.com](https://elecmarket-dz.com/categories/interrupteurs-et-prises)).
- The Algerian activity code "Fabrication de petit appareillage d'installation et d'éclairage électrique" (105504) exists, covering switches, plugs, insulators and similar. — [upgrowth.dz](https://www.upgrowth.dz/code-activite/fabrication-de-petit-appareillage-d-installation-et-d-eclairage-electrique-105504)

### Inferences
- Algeria's state-led demand (Sonelgaz grid, rail, AADL housing) flows through ENICAB, ENEL and their installers. Accessories such as cable ties, glands, junction boxes and conduit clips are probably sourced through electrical wholesalers. The best entry point is likely private-label supply to distributors (SOGEDIM type) or to local switch assemblers, rather than to the cable makers directly.

### Gaps
- I could not verify "Dima", "Brilux", "Cables de l'Est" or an "Elsewedy Algeria" plant [UNVERIFIED].
- I found no source for large electrical wholesalers in Algiers, Sétif or Oran, or for named tableautiers (panel builders). Exhibitor lists from the electricity fairs and from Batimatec (below) are the next place to look.
- No contacts were verified for ENICAB or ENEL.

## Q2. Construction consumables: quincaillerie, materials distributors, precast, tile, AADL

### Takeaway
I found no company-level sources for quincaillerie (hardware) wholesalers, precast makers, tile producers or AADL contractors in this pass. The verified, high-value route to these buyers is **Batimatec 2026 (3–7 May 2026, Algiers)**, whose exhibitor list is effectively the target list.

### Cited Findings
- Batimatec 2026, 28th edition: 3–7 May 2026 at the Palais des Expositions (SAFEX, Pins Maritimes), Algiers. Exhibitors came from about 15 countries (China, Turkey, Italy, Spain, Portugal), plus 40 start-ups. — [Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/85153); [Algérie Eco](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=239715); [ICE Italia event page](https://www.ice.it/it/area-clienti/eventi/dettaglio-evento/2026/@@/022)
- Spanish (Andalucía Trade) and Italian (ICE) trade missions attended Batimatec 2026, which confirms it as the main construction buyer and seller meeting. — [Andalucía Trade](https://www.andaluciatrade.es/eventos/mision-directa-sector-construccion-argelia-batimatec-2026/)
- **Condor + Maincor (Germany)**: JV to make industrial pipes in Algeria, a sign that Condor is entering plastic piping and building products. — [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/condor-sallie-avec-lallemand-maincor-pour-fabriquer-des-tuyaux-industriels-en-algerie/)

### Inferences
- Batimatec 2026 has already taken place. The next edition (likely around May 2027) and its catalogue are the realistic tool. Rebar spacers and tile spacers are commodities sold through quincaillerie wholesalers, so price per 1,000 units and stock availability decide the sale.

### Gaps
- I did not verify Cosider's procurement, Batimetal, named precast companies, ceramic or tile producers, or AADL contractors as buyers [UNVERIFIED]. These need a follow-up using the Batimatec catalogue or Kompass DZ, which was blocked here.

## Q3. Plumbing and irrigation

### Takeaway
Groupe Chiali (STPM Chiali, Sidi Bel Abbès) is the verified anchor for PE/PVC pipe. Several small Algerian drip-irrigation makers and installers appear in directories (Union B et CH Plastique "ISS", Anabib Plastiques at Ain Arnat, Medi Goutte at Ain Benian, Irriplast, Gharsseli Plast, Tupol, Irrinet, Agro Services). Southern greenhouse agriculture (Biskra) and the El Oued potato belt are the demand centres for drippers, connectors and fittings.

### Cited Findings

Re-check these details on the official site. — (search snippet; the source page was a directory-type result returned for the query "Groupe Chiali tubes PEHD…"; the original URL was not shown, so treat the contact as needing confirmation)

- **SARL Union B et CH Plastique (brand "ISS")** [V-dir]: Algerian maker and distributor of micro-irrigation and drip systems with local production. — [agro-iss.com/contact](https://agro-iss.com/contact/)
- **Anabib Plastiques EURL (Ain Arnat, Sétif)** [V-dir]: plastic irrigation elements and drip systems. — [Kompass DZ drip category](https://dz.kompass.com/a/systemes-d-irrigation-goutte-a-goutte-pour-l-agriculture/4814010/)
- **SIFAT (Dar El Beida, Algiers)** [V-dir]: rubber irrigation hoses. — same Kompass page
- Also listed in the same directory results, with no details verified: Irrinet, EGTPH, Agro Services, Tupol, Gharsseli Plast, Irriplast. — same Kompass page

- Demand context: drip irrigation drove rapid greenhouse growth in Biskra, supported by a network of input sellers. The Souf (El Oued) region grows more than 1 Mt of potatoes on more than 35,000 ha, about 35% of national output. — [G-EAU thesis abstract (Biskra)](https://www.g-eau.fr/index.php/en/production/theses/item/822-analysis-of-fertigation-practices-opportunities-for-improvement-through-the-use-of-participatory-modeling-in-agricultural-advice-the-case-of-greenhouse-drip-in-southern-algeria-k-laib)

### Inferences
- Drip and pipe extruders typically buy drippers and fittings, or import them from China or Turkey. A local injection molder making start connectors, tees, end plugs and online drippers could sell to these extruders and to Biskra and El Oued input dealers. Seasonality applies: sales peak before the planting campaigns.

## Q4. Appliances and electronics OEMs

### Takeaway
Condor (Bordj Bou Arréridj), Brandt/Cevital (Sétif) and Iris (Sétif) are verified, high-integration OEMs. They already make many of their own plastic parts, but government pressure on integration rates creates openings for subcontracting.

### Cited Findings
- **Condor Electronics (BBA)** [V]: produces 24 brands and accounts for 50% of Algerian appliance exports to 18 markets. Local integration runs from 45% to 100% by product line, and refrigerators are "100% algérien", including the compressor. — [Algérie Eco, 14 Jul 2026](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/07/14/condor-present-sur-18-marches-et-realise-50-des-exportations-algeriennes-en-electromenager/)
- Condor is working with Hisense and BYD on new projects in Algeria. — [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/hisense-byd-sebcondor-devoile-ses-nouveaux-projets-en-algerie/)
- **Brandt Algérie (Cevital group, Sétif)** [V]: makes most of its own parts, metal and plastic parts and PCBs, some of which are exported. Integration is 70% for refrigerators and 80% for washing machines. — [Algérie Eco, 24 Dec 2023](https://www.algerie-eco.com/2023/12/24/les-fabricants-delectromenagers-creent-une-veritable-base-industrielle-avec-des-matieres-premieres-locales/); [Horizons, Dec 2023](https://www.horizons.dz/2023/12/industrie-electromenagere-lintegration-locale-un-defi-a-relever/)
- **Iris (Sétif)** [V]: invests to stimulate local production. Iris Tyres also supplies Fiat Oran. — same Algérie Eco 2023 article

### Inferences
- Because Condor and Brandt have in-house molding, the opportunity is overflow capacity, small technical parts (knobs, clips, hinges, cable clamps) and secondary suppliers chosen to meet integration ratios. Approach the purchasing and "intégration locale" or sous-traitance departments directly.

### Gaps
- I did not verify ENIEM (Tizi Ouzou), Géant, Stream System, Raylan, Bomare or Starlight in this pass. They are known names but have no 2025–2026 source here [UNVERIFIED].

## Q5. Automotive

### Takeaway
This is the best-documented supplier programme. Stellantis Fiat El Djazair (Tafraoui, Oran) targets 30% local integration by 2026, with an aspiration of 45%, and has named local suppliers. SAREL (Algiers) is the incumbent local plastics supplier, and Sigit (Italy) supplies plastic parts with Siplast, a subsidiary of the state plastics company ENPC. A newcomer is more likely to be a Tier-2 supplier to SAREL, Sigit/Siplast or Formfleks than a direct Tier-1.

### Cited Findings
- Stellantis local partners: **SAREL** (plastic cabin components), **FERRUZ** (wheel assembly), **IRIS TYRES**, **IDE-NET** (wiring harnesses) and **GHAZAL** (LPG kits). Later additions are **Groupe SAIDANI** (tow balls) and **Megnouche Alarm**, for 7 local subcontractors in total. The target is 30% integration by 2026, with an aspiration of 45%. — [Motorsactu: fifth local subcontractor](https://motorsactu.com/stellantis-renforce-sa-presence-en-algerie-un-cinquieme-sous-traitant-local-pour-lusine-fiat-de-tafraoui/); [Algérie Eco: two new Algerian subcontractors](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=209155); [Stellantis Media ME, "Un an après son lancement…"](https://www.media.stellantis.com/me-fr/corporate/press/un-an-apres-son-lancement-l-usine-fiat-de-tafraoui-poursuit-son-developpement)
- The plant expansion adds welding and paint shops in 2025 for full CKD production. Volumes are reported as about 18–20k in 2025 with a target of 90k per year in 2026. A separate Algérie Eco headline says "60.000 véhicules seront produits en 2025". **These volume figures conflict.** — [Algérie Eco ?p=218138](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=218138); search summary of the Stellantis and Motorsactu sources
- Eleven suppliers were selected by Oct 2023: Sarel, Tosyali Algérie, Fabcom (batteries), Formfleks Algeria (acoustic insulation), ABG (windscreens), Martur (seats), Sitel (cables), Cevital, Iris Tyres, Oulad Kouider Industrie (radiators) and HTL Smart Fitting Partner. — [Algérie Eco, 20 Oct 2023](https://algerie-eco.com/2023/10/20/usine-fiat-doran-11-equipementiers-deja-selectionnes/); [Algérie Eco: liste des sous-traitants](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=182893)
- **Sigit (Italy)** signed a subcontracting deal with Stellantis El Djazair in Rome to supply plastic parts, in partnership with **Siplast (ENPC subsidiary)**. — [Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/68308); [Algérie360](https://www.algerie360.com/automobile-en-algerie-fiat-sallie-a-un-partenaire-italien-de-poids/)
- **SAREL Industries (Algiers)**: earlier a plastic-parts subcontractor to Renault Algérie, which it exported from. Partnership with Stellantis announced 9 Dec 2024. — [Algérie Eco ?p=78229](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=78229)
- Fiat Algérie has made a public call to local parts manufacturers. — [Motorsactu](https://motorsactu.com/fiat-algerie-appel-aux-fabricants-de-pieces-locales/)

### Inferences
- Small injection-molded automotive parts (clips, grommets, plugs, cable-routing clips) would likely need IATF 16949 or at least ISO 9001 and a PPAP-type process. Realistic entry is Tier-2 to SAREL, Sigit/Siplast, IDE-NET (harness clips) or Formfleks.

### Gaps
- I did not verify the current status of Renault Algérie, SNVI or Mercedes-Benz (Tiaret/Rouiba) supplier programmes in this pass.

## Q7. Packaging (closures and caps)

### Takeaway
Large food and beverage brands buy closures from established specialists. **SGT** (French, with plants in Rouiba and Sétif) is the verified incumbent for preforms and HDPE caps. Competing on closures needs high-cavitation molds and is not a "small components" niche.

### Cited Findings
- SGT has made preforms and PE caps in Algeria since 2001 (Rouiba). A second plant in Sétif covers 11,700 m² and cost €7M. SGT makes 4 billion PET preforms per year across more than 200 references. — [Algérie360](https://www.algerie360.com/specialisee-dans-la-fabrication-de-preformes-et-de-bouchons-en-polyethylenelentreprise-francaise-sgt-ouvre-une-deuxieme-usine-en-algerie/)
- Ifri introduced PET in Algeria in 1996 and initially sourced preforms from SGT France. — same source
- Cevital runs bottling (Astron project reference) and entered pharmaceutical bags. — [Astron](https://astron.biz/en/international/projects-worldwide/cevital-bottling-plant-algeria); [Algérie Eco 2018](https://algerie-eco.com/2018/09/18/lusine-produira-100-millions-de-poches-medicamenteuses-cevital-se-lance-dans-lindustrie-pharmaceutique/)

### Gaps

## Q9. How to approach buyers: roles, qualification, documents, payment, events

### Takeaway
A standard Algerian supplier file is the extract of the registre de commerce, NIF, NIS, article d'imposition, a tax-clearance extract (extrait de rôle) and current CNAS and CASNOS certificates. Trade fairs at SAFEX/Pins Maritimes and the subcontracting fairs (ALGEST, SANIST, run by BASTP and CACI) are the verified places to meet buyers. I found no source on payment terms.

### Cited Findings
- Typical tender and consultation administrative file: copy of the RC extract, NIF, extrait de rôle (apuré or with a payment schedule), and up-to-date CNAS and CASNOS certificates. — [Safqatic consultation file 04/2022](https://safqatic.dz/docs/offres/at__N___04_2022_20220207092648.pdf); [Safqatic 2019](https://safqatic.dz/docs/offres/Consultation-N-04-2019-1660.pdf)
- Registration in the RC triggers the NIF (from DGI), the NIS (from ONS) and the article d'imposition. — [upgrowth.dz NIF](https://www.upgrowth.dz/nif-algerie); [upgrowth carte fiscale](https://www.upgrowth.dz/carte-fiscale-algerie)
- **ALGEST 2025** (10th International Subcontracting Fair) was held at Pins Maritimes, Algiers, until 20 Nov 2025. It had more than 100 exhibitors and about 10,000 expected visitors, with B2B meetings. It is organised by the **Bourse Algérienne de Sous-Traitance et de Partenariat (BASTP)** and WTC Algeria. — [Algérie Eco ?p=230691](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=230691)
- **SANIST 2025** (7th National Reverse-Subcontracting Fair, run by CACI and SAFEX) took place in Algiers until 24 Apr 2025 with 70 exhibitors. Under reverse subcontracting, the large buyers exhibit their outsourcing needs. — [Algérie Eco ?p=215691](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=215691)
- Algeria has fewer than 1,000 subcontracting companies. — [Algérie Eco ?p=171328](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=171328)
- FIA 2026 (57th Foire Internationale d'Alger) ran 22–27 June 2026 at SAFEX, with Spain as guest of honour. — [Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/84917)
- SAFEX announced 60 economic events for 2026. — [Algérie Eco ?p=233687](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=233687)
- Batimatec 2026 ran 3–7 May and SIPSA-Filaha 2026 ran 18–21 May (see Q2 and Q3).

### Inferences
- **Who to target:** in OEMs (Condor, Brandt, Stellantis tier-1s), the purchasing or "achats" department and the local integration / sous-traitance manager. In SMEs (irrigation, furniture, quincaillerie), the gérant (owner).
- **Best near-term events** (all in Algiers unless noted): SANIST (around April, reverse subcontracting), ALGEST (around November), SIPSA (May), Batimatec (May) and FIA (June). The 2025–2026 editions are past, so plan for the 2026 ALGEST if it is held in November 2026 (dates not yet confirmed), and for 2027.

### Gaps
- I found no sourced data on typical payment terms (for example 30, 60 or 90 days, cheque or traite), quality certifications demanded (ISO 9001), or electricity-specific fair dates. Confirm these through interviews.
