## 1. What do trade data show for Algerian imports of plastic articles (HS 39xx) and plastic-containing parts (HS 8536/8538/8481/8424/8708/9403)?

### Takeaway
At the aggregate level Algeria imported **US$2.79 bn of plastic materials and products in 2024 and US$2.98 bn in 2025** (rising). Most of this is raw polymer. Historically about 88% of plastics imports were raw material (2016). So finished plastic articles are a smaller, unquantified share in this research. China is the #1 supplier overall and of plastic packaging, followed by Italy and Turkey. HS-4-digit values (3917/3923/3924/3925/3926, and the plastic share of 8536/8538/8481/8424/8708/9403) could not be retrieved because Comtrade, WITS, OEC and TrendEconomy were all blocked.

### Cited Findings
- Algeria's import bill for plastic materials and products: **US$2.79 bn in 2024, US$2.98 bn in 2025** (AAPI / APS figures, reported early 2026). — [APS](https://www.aps.dz/fr/economie/commerce-et-service/mm9dj2c7-enregistrement-de-675-projets-d-investissement-d-une-valeur-de-plus-de-138-milliards-de-dinars-dans-la-filiere-plastique); [Algérie Eco](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=236480)
- AAPI registered **675 plastics-sector investment projects worth DZD 138.7 bn** between 1 Feb 2022 and end-Feb 2026: 383 new projects and 292 extensions, expected to create 15,150 direct jobs. — [APS](https://www.aps.dz/fr/economie/commerce-et-service/mm9dj2c7-enregistrement-de-675-projets-d-investissement-d-une-valeur-de-plus-de-138-milliards-de-dinars-dans-la-filiere-plastique); [Echorouk](https://www.echoroukonline.com/675-مشروع-استثماري-بـ14-ألف-مليار-في-شعبة-ال)
- Algeria imported **1,095 kt of plastic raw materials in 2024**, the 3rd-largest importer in Africa and the Middle East (secondary/aggregator figure). — search snippet tied to [Plast Alger market page](https://www.plastalger.com/the-plast-market/) / [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/store/algeria-plastic-packaging-market-analysis-forecast-size-trends-and-insights/). The exact attribution was not confirmed because the page fetch was blocked.
- In 2024 China was Algeria's largest supplier of plastic packaging, then Italy, then Turkey. China, Italy and Turkey together supplied 69% of plastic bags/sacks imports. — [IndexBox plastic packaging](https://www.indexbox.io/store/algeria-plastic-packaging-market-analysis-forecast-size-trends-and-insights/); [IndexBox bags](https://www.indexbox.io/store/algeria-plastic-sacks-and-bags-market-analysis-forecast-size-trends-and-insights/)
- China held a 22.3% share of all Algerian imports in 2024, and Turkey 5.4%. — [Lloyds Bank Trade / EC factsheet search snippet](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/algeria/trade-profile)
- Historical baseline: plastics imports were **US$2.174 bn in 2016, of which US$1.904 bn was raw material**, leaving about US$270 m for finished and semi-finished plastic goods. — [Algérie Eco 2016](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=22195)
- Industry structure (older Plast Alger data): about 1,000 mostly SME companies. **About 40% are active in injection** and 20% in extrusion. Consumption was about 1 Mt/yr, half imported; per-capita use rose from 10 kg (2007) to 23 kg (2017). — [Plast Alger](https://www.plastalger.com/the-plast-market/); [Plasteurope](https://www.plasteurope.com/news/ALGERIA_t215050)
- Global context: China exported about US$32 bn of HS 3926 in 2024 and is the dominant supplier of the "other articles of plastics" category worldwide. — [OEC HS 3926](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/other-plastic-products)

### Inferences
- If raw material is still about 85–90% of the US$2.98 bn, finished plastic articles (ch. 39 excluding primary forms) might be roughly US$300–450 m/yr. This is an **ESTIMATE** extrapolated from the 2016 ratio, not a data point.
- The 2024 to 2025 rise (+6.8%) plus 675 investment projects suggests local converters are expanding fast. Commodity categories will therefore keep getting more crowded.
- Plastic sub-components hidden inside HS 8536/8538 (switches, boxes), 8481 (valves), 8424 (irrigation), 8708 (auto) and 9403 (furniture) are not separable in trade data. Policy evidence (Section 2) is the better proxy for where demand is shifting to local supply.

### Gaps
- **No HS 4/6-digit values retrieved** for 3917, 3923, 3924, 3925, 3926, 8536, 8538, 8481, 8424, 8708 or 9403 for 2022–2025. Comtrade (comtradeapi.un.org), WITS, OEC, TrendEconomy and Trade Map were blocked or not reachable. **Action for the report writer or user:** pull these manually from Trade Map (trademap.org, reporter Algeria, or mirror data from China/Turkey/Italy/Spain exports to Algeria, since Algeria's own Comtrade reporting is often lagged).
- No ONS/ALGEX/DGD (customs) publication with product-level 2024–2025 figures was found.

## 2. Government import-substitution policy 2023–2026

### Takeaway
The strongest and most recent signal is in **automotive**. In September 2026 the Prime Minister set **31 Dec 2026 as the end of imports of automotive plastic parts that can be made by the state unit GPI (Tissemsilt)**, with mandatory local sourcing from early 2027. Stellantis (Fiat Tafraoui, Oran) targets 30% local integration by 2026 and has a plastic-parts subcontracting deal (Sigit + ENPC/Siplast). Across all sectors, importers face tighter controls: a provisional import programme (PPI) filed with the ministry, plus the DAPS surtax of 30–200% on a list of about 1,095 products. Condor reports 45–100% integration in appliances.

### Cited Findings
**Automotive plastics, the key 2026 measure**
- The Prime Minister (Sifi Ghrieb) set **31 December 2026** as the deadline for importing automotive plastic parts that GPI can manufacture. Licensed vehicle makers must source locally produced plastic parts **from early 2027**, under a specification governing supply conditions. Molds must be at the plant before year-end. — [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/automobile-lalgerie-nimportera-plus-de-pieces-plastiques-des-2027/); [Radio Algérie 24 Sep 2026](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/95081); [Algérie Eco 24 Sep 2026](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/09/24/automobile-les-pieces-plastiques-fabriquees-en-algerie-ne-seront-plus-importees-des-2027/); [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/sifi-ghrieb-importation-pieces-plastiques-automobile-tissemsilt/); [Observ'Algérie](https://observalgerie.com/2026/09/25/economie/lalgerie-annonce-la-fin-de-limportation-des-pieces-plastiques-pour-voitures)
- **General Plastic Injection (GPI)** belongs to Holding Algeria Chemical Specialities (ACS), a state company. It sits in the Sidi Mansour zone, Khemisti (Tissemsilt), on more than 107,000 m², and was launched in April–May 2026. Capacity is **251,000 pieces/yr for about 200,000 vehicles**. Integration is 40% in phase 1 with a target of 70%. — [Radio Algérie EN](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/en/node/85004); [Dzair Tube 1 May 2026](https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-general-plastic-injection-unit-in-tissemsilt-to-deepen-auto-integration-drive/); [Agence Ecofin](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-industries/2709-141924-une-usine-de-composants-en-plastique-vient-renforcer-lindustrie-automobile-en-algerie)
- **Stellantis El Djazair** signed a subcontracting deal with Italian supplier **Sigit** (July 2025, Algerian-Italian summit) to produce plastic parts for Fiat Tafraoui in Algeria via **ENPC's subsidiary Siplast**. Stellantis targets 30% local integration by 2026 and wants Algerian subcontractors covering more than 30% of vehicle content. — [Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/68308); [Algérie Eco 24 Jul 2025](https://algerie-eco.com/2025/07/24/usine-fiat-doran-un-partenariat-de-sous-traitance-entre-stellantis-el-djazair-et-litalien-sigit/); [Motorsactu: Fiat call for local parts makers](https://motorsactu.com/fiat-algerie-appel-aux-fabricants-de-pieces-locales/); [Stellantis media](https://www.media.stellantis.com/me-fr/corporate/press/un-an-apres-son-lancement-l-usine-fiat-de-tafraoui-poursuit-son-developpement)
- Algérie Eco reports two new Algerian subcontractors for the Fiat Oran plant and a target of 60,000 vehicles produced in 2025. — [Algérie Eco p=209155](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=209155); [Algérie Eco p=218138](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=218138)

**General import controls**
- **DAPS** (additional provisional safeguard duty) runs at 30–200%. A Ministry of Commerce order listed **1,095 products**. Rates are mostly 30–70%, 120% for some produce and 200% for hydraulic cement. The plastic-article lines in it were not identified. — [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/importation-et-produits-daps-la-liste-des-produits-devoilee/); [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/importations-le-gouvernement-fixe-la-liste-des-produits-surtaxes)
- 2025: the Ministry of Foreign Trade and Export Promotion required **all import-for-resale companies (~300,000)** to file a **provisional import programme (PPI)** for H2 2025. The deadline was extended to 10 Aug 2025. — [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/algerie-lavenir-du-programme-previsionnel-dimportation-en-suspens/)
- An earlier precedent was the 2018 suspension of **851 products** (400 industrial), described as temporary for 2–3 years. — [Ministère du Commerce](https://commerce.gov.dz/fr/activites-ministre/levee-de-la-suspension-des-produits-a-l-importation-dans-deux-ou-trois-ans)

**Appliances / electrical / agriculture**
- **Condor** local integration ranges from 45% to 100% by product. The refrigerator is "100% Algerian" (including the compressor), heaters and cookers are 100%, washing machines are above 60% and TVs 62%. — [Afrik.com](https://www.afrik.com/condor-le-made-in-algeria-a-la-conquete-des-marches-africains); [Algérie Eco Jul 2026](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/07/14/condor-present-sur-18-marches-et-realise-50-des-exportations-algeriennes-en-electromenager/)
- Condor partnered with German **Maincor** on industrial pipes and has solar irrigation pivot projects (El-Oued pilot). — [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/condor-sallie-avec-lallemand-maincor-pour-fabriquer-des-tuyaux-industriels-en-algerie/); [Team France Export](https://www.teamfrance-export.fr/infos-sectorielles/14979/14979-pivot-solaire-pour-lirrigation-made-in-algeria-by-groupe-condor)
- The state mechanical group **AGM** started producing the first Algerian-developed irrigation pivot in May 2024. — [Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/45427)
- Drip irrigation is growing, concentrated in the Saharan regions. The target is 1 million irrigated hectares by 2027, with focus on the South. — [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/fr/industry-reports/agriculture-in-algeria); [Farmonaut](https://farmonaut.com/africa/strategies-innovantes-pour-lautosuffisance-en-ble-dur-lalgerie-releve-le-defi-de-lagriculture-durable)

### Inferences
- The auto measure creates **Tier-2 opportunities** for a small molder: clips, fasteners, grommets, caps and small trim. GPI has only 251k pieces/yr capacity across many references. That figure is very low for about 200k vehicles, so it probably counts *part references × sets*, or covers large parts only. The bulk of small clips and fasteners likely remains uncovered or is subcontracted. This is an ESTIMATE and needs verification of GPI's part list.
- The PPI and DAPS regime raises landed cost and administrative friction for imported small plastic hardware. That favors local substitutes, especially for B2B buyers (contractors, installers) who need steady supply.
- Appliance makers at high integration (Condor) likely mold large parts in-house. Opportunities lie in **spare parts / after-sales** (knobs, feet, filter caps) and Tier-2 small parts. This is an ESTIMATE.

### Gaps
- The **list of products GPI will make** (and therefore which auto plastic parts become import-banned) was not found.
- No Renault Oran restart or plastics-sourcing information was found. No information was found for Iris, Brandt/Cevital, ENIEM or Géant/Stream System on plastic parts sourcing.
- No 2023–2026 official "production nationale" list naming specific plastic articles was found. Whether DAPS covers HS 3922/3924/3926 lines is unverified.
- No ADE water meter localization data was found.

## 3. Strong local competition vs gaps; evidence on local producers of target items

### Takeaway
Local supply is **strong** for PE/PVC/PEHD pipes (Chiali and others), bottle caps/closures (SGT: 2 bn caps/yr in Sétif) and, by industry reputation, household goods, chairs, crates and buckets (about 40% of about 1,000 plastics firms do injection). **No Algerian manufacturer could be found by web search** for cable ties, wall plugs (chevilles), rebar spacers, tile spacers, electrical flush/junction boxes, cable glands, drip emitters/fittings or furniture feet. Results returned only Chinese, European or Moroccan (Ingelec) suppliers. This is *weak evidence of a gap*, not proof: small Algerian workshops are poorly indexed online. Ouedkniss prices could not be retrieved because the site needs JavaScript and fetching was blocked.

### Cited Findings
- **Closures:** **SGT Algérie SPA** (French group) has made preforms and HDPE caps since 2001 (Rouiba). In May 2018 it opened a Sétif site dedicated to caps with 7 injection presses and **2 billion caps/yr** capacity, offering 11 HDPE cap families. **Tap Chihani** also makes plastic caps by injection. — [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/specialisee-dans-la-fabrication-de-preformes-et-de-bouchons-en-polyethylenelentreprise-francaise-sgt-ouvre-une-deuxieme-usine-en-algerie/); [Novojob SGT](https://www.novojob.com/entreprise/sgt-algerie-spa/presentation); [Automa: Tap Chihani](https://automa.net/company/tap-chihani)
- **Pipes:** **Groupe Chiali / STPM Chiali** (Sidi Bel Abbès) makes all types of network pipes and exports PE pipes, for example to Mauritania (Aug 2024). — [Algérie Eco Aug 2024](https://algerie-eco.com/2024/08/22/sidi-bel-abbes-exportation-de-tubes-de-polyethylene-et-de-rond-a-beton-vers-la-mauritanie/)
- **Auto plastics:** GPI (state, Tissemsilt) and Siplast (ENPC) with Sigit are the designated local auto-plastic suppliers. — [see Section 2 sources](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/en/node/85004)
- **Electrical accessories:** search returned **Ingelec (Morocco, Casablanca, founded 1975)** as the Maghreb leader for switches, sockets, junction boxes, cable fasteners and wall plugs. No Algerian equivalent surfaced. — [Le Matin (MA)](https://lematin.ma/supplement/economie/2008/Banques_Le-tournant-au-credit-du-secteur/Ingelec_Et-la-lumiere-futa-euro�/88529/amp)
- **Directory evidence:** D&B lists many Algerian "plastics product manufacturing" companies in Ain Benian, Rouiba, Hammedi, Mezloug (Sétif) and Bir El Djir (Oran), with no product granularity. — [D&B directory](https://www.dnb.com/business-directory/company-information.plastics_product_manufacturing.dz.html?page=3)
- Searches for drip emitters/fittings, cable ties, chevilles, tile spacers and junction boxes returned only Chinese (made-in-china.com) or EU retail listings. — [made-in-china mini dripper](https://fr.made-in-china.com/factory/mini-dripper.html); [made-in-china socket box](https://fr.made-in-china.com/factory/socket-box.html)

### Inferences (ESTIMATE)
- **Crowded (avoid or low priority):** buckets, basins, garden chairs/tables, fruit/veg crates, standard bottle caps (SGT scale), PVC/PEHD pipes, standard PPR fittings, hangers, household storage.
- **Likely gaps (prioritize for verification):** electrical installation accessories (flush boxes, junction boxes, cable glands, conduit clips, saddles, cable ties); construction consumables (rebar spacers, wall plugs, tile spacers/levellers, formwork cones, insulation fixings); drip irrigation micro-parts (online emitters, start connectors, mini-valves, end plugs); furniture/hardware micro-parts (glides, edge caps, shelf supports, cam covers); auto Tier-2 clips and grommets (policy tailwind to 2027); solar mounting accessories (clips, cable clips, MC4 dust caps).
- Commoditization risk is high wherever Chinese landed price is very low and the part needs no certification (cable ties, tile spacers). The local edge must come from lead time, availability under import controls, custom colours/branding, and the DAPS/PPI cost premium.

### Gaps
- **Ouedkniss / Jumia DZ price points: none retrieved** (the JS-rendered site was blocked). All prices in the long list are ESTIMATES. Recommend manual checks on Ouedkniss for: "collier de serrage", "cheville", "cale béton", "croisillon", "boite d'encastrement", "presse étoupe", "goutteur", "patin meuble".
- No Algerian manufacturer was confirmed for any of the specifically requested items except caps (SGT, Tap Chihani) and pipes (Chiali). Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.

- **Higher value but harder:** #1 cable ties (PA66, multi-cavity hot runner), #4–6 junction boxes and glands (IP testing), #40 emitters (precision), #93 filter housings, #95–96 auto clips (IATF/PPAP), #63 pharma closures (GMP).
