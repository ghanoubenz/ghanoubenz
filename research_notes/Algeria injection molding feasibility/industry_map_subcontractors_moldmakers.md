# Algerian Plastic Injection-Molding Industry Map: Subcontractors, Mold Makers and Clusters (2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-10-03. Method note: the network proxy blocked direct page fetches for almost every Algerian domain (ouedkniss.com, kompass.com, elmouchir.caci.dz, snkplastic.com, miraf.dz, cumnewtec.com, fmpi-dz.com, 2m-expert.com, enpc-dz.com, dzair-tube.dz, yizumi.com), and zerouni-molds.com / snk-plastic.com did not resolve in DNS. All company facts below therefore come from **search-engine extracts of the cited pages**, not from full-page reads. That is why each is labelled:
- **VERIFIED (snippet)**: the claim appeared in a search extract attributed to the company's own site or an official directory and fits the other sources. Re-check it on the live page before relying on it.
- **UNVERIFIED**: there is a single weak source, the sources conflict, or the claim could not be confirmed.
"Date" means the date the page was retrieved through search (2026-10-03) unless a publication date is given.

## 1. The seven named companies: what they actually do

### Takeaway
Of the seven, **SNK Plastic (Rouiba)** is the only clear "bring your own mold" injection subcontractor. **MIRAF Industrie (Bordj Bou Arréridj)** is a molder plus mold maker that also does aluminium casting. **Rampton (Rouiba)**, **Precision Industry (Ouled Moussa, Boumerdès)** and **Cumnewtec (El Eulma, Sétif)** are mold makers / precision machinists. **2M Expert (Oued Smar)** and **AFC Industry (Kouba)** are machine distributors, not molders.

### Inferences
- For "here's my mold, run 5k/20k/100k pieces", SNK Plastic is the best-documented candidate (50–650 t covers most consumer and technical parts up to about 2 kg). MIRAF is a second candidate in the BBA electronics/appliance cluster.
- 2M Expert (Cosmos) and AFC Industry are useful for buying machines and for referrals to their customer base of molders, but they do not mold parts.
- Precision Industry, Rampton and Cumnewtec are candidate local mold makers. Their claimed capabilities (EDM, tolerances) come from their own marketing.

### Gaps
- None of the seven publish MOQs, price lists, machine brands (except 2M Expert / Cosmos) or material lists.
- Live pages could not be fetched. Phone numbers and addresses are as shown in search extracts and should be re-checked.
- I could not confirm whether Rampton, Precision Industry or Cumnewtec run injection presses (for mold tryout or production).

## 2. Other Algerian injection molders and mold makers, by cluster

### Takeaway
There are four documented clusters. (1) **Sétif / El Eulma**: the public ENPC group (SIPLAST, ALMOULES) and several private mold makers. (2) **Bordj Bou Arréridj**: Condor plus MIRAF and the appliance ecosystem. (3) **East Algiers / Boumerdès** (Rouiba, Oued Smar, Kouba, Tassala El Merdja, Ouled Moussa, Khemis El Khechna): SNK, distributors and several mold makers. (4) The new state **automotive plastics** plant GPI in Tissemsilt. Large pipe/extrusion groups (Chiali, K-Plast) are mainly extrusion, not custom molding.

## 3. Mold-making capability, prices, lead times and technical centers

### Takeaway
Local mold makers advertise CNC and EDM (Zerouni, Precision Industry) and CAD/CNC (ALMOULES, FMPI). The only published lead time found is **SIPLAST's 6 weeks** for injection molds. No mold prices were published. The **CDTA** (Baba Hassen) offers rapid prototyping and 3D printing.

### Cited Findings
- SIPLAST (ENPC) quotes a 6-week lead time for injection molds — [ENPC SIPLAST](https://www.enpc-dz.com/siplast/)
- Zerouni Molds: CNC milling and turning, EDM, heat treatment; molds for plastic, aluminium and zamak injection — [zerouni-molds.com](https://zerouni-molds.com/)
- Precision Industry: CNC and conventional machining, EDM; injection, blow and thermoforming molds — [precisionindustry-dz.com](https://precisionindustry-dz.com/)
- ALMOULES: CAD design office and CNC — [ENPC ALMOULES](http://www.enpc-dz.com/almoules/)
- FMPI: "state-of-the-art CNC fleet"; also sells equipment and software and provides training — [fmpi-dz.com](https://www.fmpi-dz.com/equipements-fabrication-industrie/)
- **CDTA** (Centre de Développement des Technologies Avancées, Cité 20 Août 1956, Baba Hassen, Algiers): prototyping platform, 3D printing, rapid prototyping (Stratoconception®), reverse engineering and additive manufacturing, plus training — [CDTA contact](https://www.cdta.dz/en/?p=12201)
- Universities (UMMTO Tizi Ouzou, Béjaïa, Laghouat, El Oued) publish many master's theses on injection mold design. This shows an academic skills pipeline, not commercial services — [UMMTO](https://dspace.ummto.dz/bitstreams/b4209db8-fbc4-4b4c-8e18-56a41fec2844/download); [Univ Béjaïa](https://univ-bejaia.dz/jspui/bitstream/123456789/20404/1/Etude%20de%20conception%20et%20analyse%20de%20fabrication%20d%e2%80%99un%20moule.pdf)
- Molds for plastic injection come under customs heading **8480.71** — [ConformePro tariff](https://conformepro.dz/resources/tarif-douanier/sous-position/84.80.710000/)
- The PM required the molds for GPI's automotive parts to be on site before the end of 2026 — [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/automobile-lalgerie-nimportera-plus-de-pieces-plastiques-des-2027/); [Observ'Algérie](https://observalgerie.com/2026/09/25/economie/lalgerie-annonce-la-fin-de-limportation-des-pieces-plastiques-pour-voitures)

### Inferences
- The tight deadline for GPI's molds suggests high-end automotive molds will be largely imported (China, Turkey, Europe). Local shops more likely cover simpler molds, mold repair and maintenance.
- Mold quality reputation could not be sourced. No independent reviews were found.

### Gaps
- No published prices or lead times except SIPLAST's. No wire-EDM confirmation for any local shop. No CRAPC, CNRC or ENSTP mold services found.

## 4. Associations, trade fairs and subcontracting exchanges

### Takeaway
The main platforms are **Plast Alger** (10th edition, 30 Mar–1 Apr 2026, SAFEX), **ALGEST** (industrial subcontracting, 23–25 Nov 2026, SAFEX) and the **BASTP** subcontracting exchange (about 250–300 members, including plastics). ## 5. Competition: where it is intense and where niches remain

### Takeaway
Commodity injection (household goods, chairs, buckets, crates), pipes and fittings, and packaging/preforms are crowded and price-driven. **Automotive plastic parts** are a policy-created niche from 2027 (import ban on locally makeable parts), but the state-owned GPI is positioned to capture it. Technical, electrical and appliance parts outside the captive OEM plants appear less served.

### Cited Findings
- Sector size: about 1,000 mostly SME firms, split roughly 40% injection, 40% packaging/plasticulture and 20% extrusion — [Plasteurope](https://www.plasteurope.com/news/ALGERIA_t215050); 580 plastics and rubber firms with more than 10,000 employees, dominated by K-Plast and STPM; industrial subcontracting is "underdeveloped" — [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/industrie-plastique-filiere-a-recherche-dinvestisseurs/); older estimate of about 2,200 firms / 20,000 workers in 2012 (same 40/20/40 split) — search extract (UNVERIFIED; source not cleanly attributed)
- Household plastics (tables, chairs, boxes, buckets, basins): basic models saturated; competition is on price, against Chinese imports and counterfeits; informal under-priced imports are about 15% of supply. This is a Maghreb-wide article, so Algerian applicability is **partly inferred** — [La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/affaires/articles-menagers-en-plastique-la-concurrence-asiatique-menace-un-marche-en-plein-boom-22729/)
- Automotive: from **1 Jan 2027**, licensed carmakers and assemblers must source plastic parts that GPI can make locally; imports of such parts are banned after 31 Dec 2026, with a specification (cahier des charges) to frame supply — [Algérie Eco 24/09/2026](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/09/24/automobile-les-pieces-plastiques-fabriquees-en-algerie-ne-seront-plus-importees-des-2027/); [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/automobile-lalgerie-nimportera-plus-de-pieces-plastiques-des-2027/)
- An academic study on competitive intensity and technological potential in the Algerian plastics sector exists — [ASJP CERIST](https://asjp.cerist.dz/en/downArticle/127/14/1/174241) (not read in full)

### Inferences
- **Intense**: household items, chairs, crates, buckets, PEHD/PVC pipes and fittings (Chiali, K-Plast), and packaging and preforms (many players plus AAPI's pipeline of 675 projects).
- **Niches**: precision technical parts (1 g–2 kg), electrical and electronic housings for the BBA ecosystem, Tier-2 automotive parts that GPI does not cover, short-run and export-oriented subcontracting (SNK's model), and mold maintenance/repair.
- GPI may itself need Tier-2 subcontractors and mold makers. This is a possible opportunity, not evidence.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on capacity utilisation, price per machine-hour, or the share of custom molders versus own-product molders.
