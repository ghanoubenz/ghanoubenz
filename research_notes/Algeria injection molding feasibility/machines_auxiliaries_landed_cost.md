Further ESTIMATES:
- **Nozzle:** sphere radius is usually SR10 at 60–160 t, and the locating ring is Ø100 mm (Ø125 mm on some 160 t machines). This must be matched with the mold maker.
- **Energy use:** a servo-hydraulic machine typically uses about 0.4–0.6 kWh/kg of PP, and a fixed-displacement pump machine about 0.8–1.2 kWh/kg. A servo saves 30–60% of hydraulic drive energy, depending on cycle and hold time.

### Inferences
- **Max practical part weight (ESTIMATE, industry rule):** use about 30–80% of the rated shot capacity.
  - Below about 20–30%, residence time is too long and the material degrades.
  - Above about 80%, there is no cushion and the process becomes unstable.
  - Shot weight is quoted in PS. PP and HDPE shot weight ≈ 0.85–0.9 × the PS figure (melt density about 0.73–0.75 vs about 0.93–0.95 for PS).
  - Example: an MA1200 with a 290 g PS shot ≈ 250 g PP. The practical shot (all parts plus runner) is about 75–200 g PP.
- **Tonnage check (ESTIMATE):** clamping force ≈ projected area × 0.3–0.5 t/cm² for PP thin walls. A 150 cm² part therefore needs about 45–75 t, and tie-bar spacing often limits mold size before tonnage does.
- For a single-machine startup, 120 t (410 mm tie-bars) covers a broader range of molds than 80 t at only a small cost premium. 150–160 t is justified if buckets or crates over 300 g are planned.

## 4. Landed cost: direct from China vs Algerian distributor vs used in Algeria

### Takeaway
A 120 t new Chinese servo machine at about USD 14k FOB lands in Algeria at roughly USD 22–27k all-in, before AAPI exemptions. That assumes about 2–6% duty and 19% VAT, with VAT recoverable for a VAT-registered producer. A local distributor typically adds a 15–35% margin but includes commissioning and warranty. Freight to Algiers is volatile: USD 2.9–5.1k per 40ft typical, with 2026 spikes up to about USD 9–11k.

## 5. Algerian rules on importing machinery (used/renovated, importer status, AAPI incentives)

### Takeaway
Used production equipment may be imported only if it is under 5 years old (from manufacture or first commissioning), under Executive Decree 24-241 of 22 July 2024 (JORA no. 50). Further conditions:
- It must have an accredited conformity/working-order certificate.
- It must be put into service within 6 months.
- It is imported by the producing operator for its own use.

AAPI registration under Law 22-18 gives customs duty and VAT exemption on equipment during the realization phase.

### Cited Findings
- **Executive Decree no. 24-241 of 22/07/2024**, JORA no. 50, covers the conditions for customs clearance of used production lines/equipment and used agricultural equipment — VERIFIED (title/URL via search) [douane.gov.dz PDF](https://douane.gov.dz/IMG/pdf/decret_executif_no_24-241_du_22_07_2024_fr-2.pdf)
- Production lines and equipment are eligible if they do **not exceed 5 years from date of manufacture** or, failing that, from date of commissioning. The age limit for agricultural equipment is 7 years — VERIFIED (snippet) [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-autorise-limportation-du-materiel-agricole-de-moins-de-7-ans/); [Djazagro 2024-09-12](https://news.djazagro.com/article/2024/09/12/algerie/)
- Good working order must be attested by a conformity assessment body accredited by ALGERAC or by a recognized foreign accreditation body — VERIFIED (snippet) [same sources](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/49362)
- Equipment must be put into service by the operator within **6 months** of clearance, recorded in a report by a bailiff (huissier) or a sworn approved expert — VERIFIED (snippet) [Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/49362); [Liberté](https://www.liberte-algerie.com/actualite/de-nouvelles-regles-a-l-importation-359836)
- Background on the earlier "équipements rénovés" regime and easing of the authorization procedure: [Liberté](https://www.liberte-algerie.com/economie/allegement-de-la-procedure-d-octroi-d-autorisation-357358); [TSA mode d'emploi](https://www.tsa-algerie.dz/importation-dequipements-renoves-mode-demploi/amp/); [gitut blog](https://dz-fr.gitut.com/blog/limportation-de-chanes-de-production-et-quipements-rnovs/)
- A separate regime allows temporary importation of industrial equipment under conditions — [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-autorise-sous-conditions-limportation-temporaire-dequipements-industriels/)
- **Investment Law 22-18 (2022):**
  - Investments must be registered with AAPI before realization.
  - Registration gives an on-the-spot certificate with a list of eligible goods and services.
  - The realization phase brings exemption from customs duties and VAT on eligible equipment, plus registration fee exemptions.
  - Realization phase lasts 3 years (Sectors regime) or 5 years (Zones and Structuring regimes).

  VERIFIED (snippets) [Analyse loi 22-18](https://investirenalgerie.com/analyse-loi-22-18-investissement-algerie/); [JORA no. 60 application texts](https://mfa.gov.dz/media/Fr_textes_dapplication_de_la_loi_sur_linvestissement.pdf); [MFDGI avantages](https://www.mfdgi.gov.dz/fr/professionnels/avantages-investissement/investissements-et-emplois/avantages-investissement); [upgrowth AAPI 2026](https://www.upgrowth.dz/andi)
- About 160 production and service activities are excluded from these advantages (a negative list) — [Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/14893). It was not checked whether plastic injection is on the list (ESTIMATE: plastics transformation is normally eligible).

### Inferences
- A startup that registers with AAPI before ordering can import a new machine and auxiliaries duty- and VAT-free. Exemption here means cash saved, not just recoverable VAT.
- A new SARL/EURL with a production activity code (NAA manufacturing code in the RC) can import equipment for its own use. It does not need "import-for-resale" status, which is a separate and heavily regulated activity (ESTIMATE / verify with CNRC).
- Importing a used Haitian from China or Europe is legal only if the machine is under 5 years old with an accredited inspection certificate. That makes cheap 8–15-year-old European machines non-importable.

## 6. Auxiliary equipment: price ranges and priority for PP/HDPE

### Takeaway
For PP and HDPE (non-hygroscopic), a dehumidifying dryer is unnecessary. The day-one must-haves are:
- mold cooling (a chiller; Algeria's summer heat makes air-cooled chillers marginal)
- a hopper loader
- a simple hopper heater/dryer for surface moisture
- a granulator for sprues and rejects
- a mixer or doser for masterbatch
- basic QC tools

Total about USD 8–18k from China (ESTIMATE).

  - basic USD 150–600; auxiliary versions USD 300–2,000

## 7. Electricity: Sonelgaz industrial tariff and connection

### Takeaway
The MT (medium-voltage) industrial tariff 41 has an energy price of about 1.16 DZD/kWh (116.15 cDA/kWh) plus a fixed monthly fee and a power charge. Tariff 44 is about 1.81 DZD/kWh. Algerian industrial power is very cheap: about USD 0.01 per kWh at the energy-only official rate.

### Cited Findings
- MT tariff code 41: fixed monthly fee 38,673.35 DA; power price 25.85 DA/month/kW; active energy 116.15 cDA/kWh — VERIFIED (snippet) [AAPI "Coût des facteurs"](https://aapi.dz/cout-des-facteurs-de-lalgerie/); [infoelec.dz tariffs](https://www.infoelec.dz/article/tarifs-delectricite-et-de-gaz.html); [algerie-electricite.com/tarifs](https://algerie-electricite.com/tarifs)
- MT tariff code 44: fixed fee 515.65 DA/month; power 38.70 DA/month/kW; energy 180.58 cDA/kWh — VERIFIED (snippet, same sources)
- The 2025 changes concern mainly household tax and fee changes — [algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/vos-factures-sonelgaz-augmenteront-en-2025-nous-vous-revelons-les-montants-du-nouvel-impot/)

### Inferences
- **Energy cost per kg (ESTIMATE):** a servo machine at about 0.5 kWh/kg plus auxiliaries at about 0.3 kWh/kg gives about 0.8 kWh/kg, so roughly 1–2 DZD/kg of PP. That is negligible against resin cost of about 200+ DZD/kg.
  - Servo-vs-fixed-pump savings are therefore small in DZD terms.
  - The real benefits of servo are lower oil temperature (a smaller chiller load), quieter running, and the ability to work on a weaker connection (lower kVA).
- **BT vs MT:** about 60–80 kVA can often be served on BT (low voltage, 400 V) in an industrial zone. Above about 100–250 kVA, Sonelgaz typically requires an MT connection with a client-owned transformer station (ESTIMATE). An MT post with a 160–250 kVA transformer typically costs several million DZD and can take months; NEEDS QUOTATION from SDC/Sonelgaz Distribution.
