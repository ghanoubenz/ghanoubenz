## 1. Chinese, Turkish and Algerian mold prices and lead times for simple small parts

### Takeaway
Simple small-part molds from China (P20/718H, LKM base, cold runner, 1–4 cavities) generally sit in the low thousands to about US$15k. Hot runners add US$3k–15k, and S136 adds 40–60% over P20. Online cap-mold listings start at about US$800–10,000, but these are often low-spec. Chinese T1 lead time is commonly quoted at 25–45 working days. No 2024–2026 published price data was found for Turkish or Algerian moldmakers, so those need direct quotations.

## 2. SPI mold classes, mold life by steel, and verifying steel and build quality

### Takeaway
SPI classes rank molds by expected life: 101 is over 1M cycles, 102 is 500k–1M, 103 is under 500k, 104 is under 100k, and 105 is under 500 (prototype). Class 101 requires hardened cavities (at least 48 HRC). Verify the steel with mill test certificates plus your own hardness and XRF checks. Contractually demand the handover documents.

### Cited Findings
- SPI cycles: 101 over 1,000,000; 102 500k–1M; 103 under 500k; 104 under 100k; 105 under 500 cycles. VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [Texas Injection Molding, SPI classifications](https://texasinjectionmolding.com/society-of-the-plastic-industry-mold-building-standards/); [Plastikon SPI class summary PDF](https://www.plastikon.com/wp-content/uploads/spi-class-summary.pdf)
- Hardness requirements by class:
  - Class 101: cavity and core at least 48 HRC; other components at least 28 HRC
  - Class 103: cavity and core at least 28 HRC; structure at least 18 HRC
  - Classes 101/102 are often hardened to 50+ HRC
  - Classes 104/105 commonly use P20, 718, 738, 618, 2311, 2711
  - Higher-grade options include 8407, S136, 420, H13, 2316, 2344, SKD61, DC53

  VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [Texas Injection Molding](https://texasinjectionmolding.com/society-of-the-plastic-industry-mold-building-standards/); [Firstmold mold lifespan](https://firstmold.com/tips/mold-lifespan/); [3ERP mold life](https://www.3erp.com/blog/injection-mold-life-expectancy/)
- Mill Test Reports (MTRs) link the steel to its chemistry, hardness and heat-treatment history. Typical hardness ranges: H13 48–52 HRC, P20 28–34 HRC. MTRs cost about US$200–500 per steel order. VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [Boxu, steel certificates vs none](https://www.boxumold.com/comparisons/steel-certificates-vs-no-certificates)
- Inspectors check that the factory has XRF (chemistry) plus hardness testing to confirm the grade, and ideally a CMM. VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [QualityInspection.org (Sofeast), Inspections of moulds and tools in China](https://qualityinspection.org/moulds-tools-china/)
- XRF is a fast, non-destructive secondary check on the MTR. VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [Evident Scientific, MTR guide](https://ims.evidentscientific.com/en/insights/mill-test-reports-in-metal-manufacturing-a-comprehensive-guide)
- A public mold design review checklist exists. It covers:
  - tie-bar and platen fit
  - locating ring (3.990" Ø in the US example) and a ½" sphere nozzle radius
  - ejector size and location
  - mold base brand, number and hardness
  - support pillars
  - water nozzles (recessed no more than 3 mm)

  VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [Texas Injection Molding, Mold Design Review Checklist PDF](https://texasinjectionmolding.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Mold-Design-Review-Checklist.pdf)
- Firstmold publishes an Injection Mold Acceptance Standards Guide that can serve as an acceptance checklist. VERIFIED (exists in search, 2026-10-03; contents not read) — [Firstmold](https://firstmold.com/tips/injection-mold-acceptance-standards-guide/)

### Inferences
- Practical steel verification on a budget (ESTIMATE / best practice):
  1. Require the steel brand and grade in the contract (e.g., ASSAB 718H, LKM P20, or "S136H from ASSAB/Uddeholm, not generic 2083").
  2. Require the mill certificate plus a photo of the steel block with its heat number and the supplier tag at rough machining.
  3. Have your agent or a third-party inspector do a portable Leeb or Rockwell hardness check on the inserts. An XRF gun reading by an inspection firm costs about 1 man-day.
  4. Look for engraved steel and hardness stamps on the inserts.
- A Chinese 718H mold sold as "S136" is a common substitution risk. XRF tells them apart easily (S136 has about 13% Cr; 718 has about 2% Cr plus Ni). ESTIMATE, based on known alloy chemistries.
- Handover package to require in the contract (ESTIMATE, industry practice):
  - final 3D mold design (STEP) and 2D assembly / BOM
  - steel certificates and heat-treatment certificate
  - cooling diagram
  - hot-runner wiring diagram and HRS brand certificate
  - spare parts list plus physical spares (ejector pins, springs, O-rings, a nozzle tip)
  - Moldflow report
  - T1 and final sample dimensional report (CMM for critical dimensions)
  - process sheet from the final trial (machine tonnage, temperatures, pressures, cycle)
  - trial video
  - mold photos

## 3. Payment milestones and contract clauses (China)

### Takeaway
Never prepay more than 30–50%. Hold at least 30% until T1 or final samples are approved. Spell out mold ownership, the warranty and the modification policy in writing, and pair the contract with a China-enforceable NNN agreement.

### Cited Findings
- The deposit should be no more than 30–50%, and at least 30% should be paid only after acceptable T1 samples are confirmed. VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [RJC Mold, China injection molds purchasing](https://rjcmold.com/blog/china-injection-molds-purchasing/)
- A common split is 50/40/10: 50% deposit, 40% at first functional sample (T1) approval, 10% after golden sample approval. VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [NewBuyingAgent wiki, Sample & Mold fees](https://www.newbuyingagent.com/wiki/sample-mold-fees)
- The contract should state that the mold is the buyer's property, or the factory may refuse to release it. The factory should be responsible for maintenance, cleaning and insured storage while the mold is at its premises. Many contracts include a mold warranty (free repair). VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [ChinaImportal, Buying injection molds in China](https://www.chinaimportal.com/?p=4731); [NewBuyingAgent](https://www.newbuyingagent.com/wiki/sample-mold-fees)
- T1 samples should be production-equivalent (including surface treatment), and mass production starts only after written approval. VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [ChinaImportal](https://www.chinaimportal.com/?p=4731)
- Firstmold publishes standard terms and conditions, including payment terms, in several languages. VERIFIED (exists, 2026-10-03; contents not read) — [Firstmold T&C](https://firstmold.com/de/terms-and-conditions/)

### Inferences (ESTIMATE / best practice)
- Recommended structure for an export mold: 30% deposit, 30% at T1 samples with a dimensional report, and 40% after final sample approval plus a pre-shipment mold inspection (by your agent).
- 40/30/30 is an acceptable fallback. Avoid 50/50 or "100% before T1".
- Note the Algerian constraint in §7. The 120% prior domiciliation provision and the rule that shipment must follow domiciliation may force an L/C, or a transfer at shipment, for the balance. Agree in advance with your Algerian bank how the deposit (an advance payment before shipment) will be authorised.
- Clauses to include:
  1. Buyer owns the mold and its design files; tooling is not to be used for others.
  2. Steel and mold-base brand and grade named per part, with no substitution without written consent.
  3. Free modifications until sample approval when the defect is the maker's (dimensions out of the agreed tolerance, flash, short shots, ejection marks); buyer-requested design changes are quoted separately.
  4. Warranty of N shots (e.g., 300k for P20, 500k–1M for hardened steel) or 12 months, covering wear parts.
  5. Delay penalty (e.g., 0.5–1% per week, capped at 10%).
  6. NNN agreement: non-disclosure, non-use, non-circumvention. It must be written in Chinese under Chinese law and a Chinese court, signed with the company chop, to be enforceable in China.
  7. Final trial in the presence of the agent, with video.
  8. Export packing specification (see §5).
- The NNN details are an ESTIMATE based on widely known China IP practice. Not sourced in this session.

- No primary legal source (e.g., China IPR SME Helpdesk) was retrieved for NNN wording in this session.

## 5. Shipping molds from China to Algeria: rates, transit, customs, documents, packing

### Takeaway
Air cargo to Algiers was about US$4–8/kg in 2025–26 with 9–11 days transit. LCL sea freight was about US$90–210/CBM base, with 38–45 days transit (plus 3–7 days for LCL handling). Courier (DHL/FedEx) is only practical for small molds, and Algerian customs restricts express imports with a commercial character. HS 8480.71 covers injection molds for plastics, but the exact Algerian duty rate needs checking on douane.gov.dz. VAT is 19%. Packing should be anti-rust oil plus a VCI bag in an ISPM-15 crate.

## 6. Air versus sea economics; DFM, Moldflow and trial rounds

### Takeaway
Sea LCL takes about 6–8 weeks door to door, against about 2 weeks by air. For a 300–500 kg mold the air premium is roughly US$1–3k. That pays off if a month of earlier production is worth more than that premium in contribution margin, which is usually true for a running SKU. Moldflow costs about US$2k–5k if billed separately, while DFM is usually free or included.

### Cited Findings
- Air transit is 9–11 days; LCL is 38–45 days plus 3–7 days. VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [Sino-Shipping](https://www.sino-shipping.com/freight-china-algeria/); [Dantful](https://www.dantful.com/container-shipping-costs-from-china-to-algeria/)
- A full Moldflow analysis costs US$2,000–5,000 and takes 5–15 business days. A DFM review costs US$500–2,000 or is often included, takes 2–5 days, and catches 60–80% of issues. Many Chinese makers offer DFM plus basic mold-flow for free. VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [Boxu, DFM vs Moldflow](https://www.boxumold.com/comparisons/dfm-vs-moldflow); [RJC Mold services](https://rjcmold.com/services/mold-making)
- DFM covers wall thickness, draft, undercuts, ejection, gate, parting line and material. Moldflow (FEA) covers fill time, pressure, weld lines, air traps, shrinkage, cooling and warpage. VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [Boxu](https://www.boxumold.com/comparisons/dfm-vs-moldflow)

### Inferences (ESTIMATE)
- Breakeven rule: air pays off when (days saved, about 30–35) × daily contribution margin from the mold > air premium (about US$1,000–3,000 for 300–500 kg).
  - Example: 4 cavities × 20 s cycle × 20 h/day ≈ 14,400 parts/day. At only US$0.01 margin per part that is about US$144/day, or about US$4.6k over 32 days, so air wins.
  - Sea makes sense for molds of products not yet sold, for heavy molds over 1 t, or when shipping together with machines in FCL.
- Trial definitions (industry usage, not sourced here):
  - T0: first internal shot without the customer, often without texture.
  - T1: first samples sent to the customer, with a dimensional report.
  - T2/T3: after corrections.
  - Final / golden sample: approval for shipment.
  - Typical simple molds need 2–3 rounds (T1 plus 1–2 corrections). Budget 1–2 weeks per correction loop, and DHL the samples to Algeria (small parcel, sample status).

## 7. Mold-machine matching and the mold specification template

### Takeaway
The mold specification must reproduce the press interface data exactly:
- locating ring Ø
- nozzle sphere radius and orifice
- tie-bar clearance and platen size
- min/max mold height and opening stroke
- ejector KO pattern, thread and stroke
- clamping method (slots / T-slots / screw holes / magnetic)
- water connector type and size
- hot-runner controller zones, voltage and connector pinout

  - water nozzle recess of no more than 3 mm

## 8. Algeria: foreign exchange and payment for imports of goods and services

### Takeaway
Since Banque d'Algérie Note n°01/DGC/2026 (14 May 2026), all goods imports must be domiciled at an Algerian bank **before shipment**. The domiciliation must be at least 30 days ahead, with a **120% provision**, and transport documents must be dated after domiciliation. Service imports (e.g., mold design) also need domiciliation and pay the domiciliation tax. Payment abroad goes only through the domiciliating bank. Paying small amounts "on the side" is not a legal channel for a company.

### Cited Findings
- Note aux banques n°01/DGC/2026 (14 May 2026):
  - all goods imports require prior bank domiciliation before any shipment by the foreign supplier
  - banks must check that transport and shipment documents are dated after domiciliation
  - no domiciliation is accepted after the effective shipment, except in duly justified and expressly authorised cases
  - a 120% financial provision is required at domiciliation, at least 30 days before shipment

  VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [Banque d'Algérie, Note 01/DGC/2026 (PDF)](https://www.bank-of-algeria.dz/stoodroa/2026/05/note-01-DGC-2026.pdf); [Algérie-Eco, 14 May 2026](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/05/14/importations-la-banque-dalgerie-impose-la-domiciliation-bancaire-avant-lexpedition-des-marchandises/); [Maghreb Émergent](https://maghrebemergent.news/fr/domiciliation-bancaire-la-banque-dalgerie-impose-de-nouvelles-regles-aux-importateurs/); [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/la-banque-dalgerie-fixe-de-nouvelles-regles-pour-les-importations/)
- A related May 2026 Banque d'Algérie rule sets new financial-capacity requirements for importers who resell goods as is. This is less relevant to a manufacturer importing its own tooling. VERIFIED (title only, 2026-10-03) — [Algérie-Eco, 19 May 2026](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/05/19/importations-pour-la-revente-en-letat-la-banque-dalgerie-fixe-de-nouvelles-exigences-en-matiere-de-surface-financiere-des-operateurs/)

- Règlement 2021-01 (28 March 2021) amends Règlement 2007-01 on current transactions:
  - goods and services import and export contracts require mandatory domiciliation
  - service imports that lead to transfers abroad pay the domiciliation tax (TDB)
  - payment of imports goes exclusively through the domiciliating approved intermediary bank

  VERIFIED (snippet, 2026-10-03) — [Banque d'Algérie, Règlementation des changes](https://www.bank-of-algeria.dz/devises-reglement/); [Règlement 07-01 on douane.gov.dz](https://www.douane.gov.dz/IMG/pdf/reglement_no07-01_du_09012007_de_la_banque_d_algerie.pdf); [TSA Algérie](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/transferts-de-devises-ce-qui-change-en-algerie/)

### Inferences (ESTIMATE; confirm with the domiciliating bank)
- Practical impact on mold purchases:
  - Open the domiciliation and block 120% of the mold value in DZD at least 30 days before the mold ships.
  - The Chinese maker's 30% deposit at order is a "paiement anticipé". Whether and how the bank will transfer it before shipment depends on the bank and the payment instrument (transfer, remise documentaire, L/C). Several Algerian banks historically favour or require documentary instruments for goods.
  - Plan the timeline: order, then domiciliate at least 30 days before T-final, then ship. Air freight does not shorten this 30-day banking lead.
- Mold design or DFM bought separately ("importation de services"):
  - It requires a service contract or invoice plus domiciliation.
  - It may trigger Algerian withholding tax on payments to non-residents (IRG/IBS retenue à la source, often cited as 30% for services; NOT verified here) and a tax clearance before transfer.
  - Simplest option: have the design, DFM and Moldflow included in the mold price (one goods contract).
- Paying small amounts through the agent's personal account, or in cash or parallel-market euros, is outside the legal framework for a company. It also leaves no proof of payment for customs valuation and VAT. Not advisable.
