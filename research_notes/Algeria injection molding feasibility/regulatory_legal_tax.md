## 1. Company formation (legal form, registration, activity codes, cost, timeline)

### Takeaway
A plastic injection plant must be set up as a commercial entity registered at the CNRC (a personne physique commerçant, EURL or SARL). The auto-entrepreneur status (Law 22-23) excludes manufacturing. Plastic-article manufacturing codes in the CNRC nomenclature (group 1113, e.g. 111305 / 111309 / 111310) are reported to be "free" activities that need no prior ministerial approval. Setting up a SARL reportedly costs about 70k–130k DZD and takes about 3–8 weeks.

### Cited Findings
- SARL minimum capital is 100,000 DZD, divided into equal shares. Total creation time is 21–42 business days, of which CNRC registration takes 7–15 days. Total cost is about 70,682–130,632 DZD: name reservation (negative certificate via Sijilcom) 800 DZD, notarial statutes 30,000–80,000 DZD, BOAL publication 7,610 DZD, CNRC registration 9,472–10,112 DZD. VERIFIED-snippet — [UpGrowth: création SARL / coût 2026](https://www.upgrowth.dz/simulateur-cout-creation-entreprise-2026); [UpGrowth checklist](https://www.upgrowth.dz/checklist-creation-sarl)
- CNRC activity codes for plastics fall under sous-groupe 1113: 111305 "Fabrication d'articles en plastique N.D.A", 111309 "Fabrication de tout corps creux en plastique et en tout type de résine", 111310 "Fabrication d'emballages en plastique". 111309 is described as a FREE activity, open after simple CNRC registration with no prior ministerial approval. VERIFIED-snippet — [UpGrowth code 111309](https://www.upgrowth.dz/code-activite/fabrication-de-tout-corps-creux-en-plastique-et-en-tout-type-de-resine-111309); [UpGrowth code 111310](https://www.upgrowth.dz/code-activite/fabrication-d-emballages-en-plastique-111310)
- Related codes exist for plastic construction materials ("Fabrication de matériaux de construction en plastique", 109111) and plastic furniture (110206). There are also import codes for producers: 433101 "importation d'équipements et matériels destinés à la fabrication…" and 433201 "importation de matières premières destinées à la fabrication…". VERIFIED-snippet — [UpGrowth 109111](https://www.upgrowth.dz/code-activite/fabrication-de-materiaux-de-construction-en-plastique-109111); [UpGrowth 433201](https://www.upgrowth.dz/code-activite/importation-de-matieres-premieres-destinees-a-la-fabrication-et-la-433201); [UpGrowth 433101](https://www.upgrowth.dz/code-activite/importation-dequipements-et-materiels-destines-a-la-fabrication-et-la-433101)
- Auto-entrepreneur (Law 22-23 of 18 Dec 2022): turnover ceiling 5,000,000 DZD/year. Manufacturing, retail, wholesale and restaurants are not eligible. IFU is 0.5% of turnover with a 10,000 DZD/year minimum. An optional flat CASNOS contribution of 24,000 DZD/year is available. Status is lost only after the ceiling is exceeded 3 consecutive years (art. 13). VERIFIED-snippet (aggregator) — [UpGrowth ANAE pages, via search](https://www.upgrowth.dz/activite-anae/technicien-de-maintenance-industrielle-063120)
- Since the PPI (2025–2026), raw-material import programmes apply to companies holding activity codes in **sections 01 (production) and 07**. VERIFIED-snippet — [TSA: PPI reconduit pour 2026](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/importations-lalgerie-reconduit-le-ppi-pour-2026-avec-une-nouveaute/)

### Inferences
- Use a SARL/EURL (or a personne physique commerçant) with a section-01 production code, e.g. 111305 plus any specific codes (111309, 111310, 109111). Add 433101/433201 to import machines and resin for own use.
- After the CNRC: NIF (DGI), NIS (ONS), article d'imposition, CNAS employer affiliation, and CASNOS for the gérant/non-salaried partners. ASSUMPTION: these are now largely chained through the CNRC / one-stop process, with each step taking a few days.
- Subcontracting: a production-coded company can probably outsource some operations (e.g. mold making) while still doing "production". No explicit rule was found. ASSUMPTION.
- SPA: ASSUMPTION — Commercial Code minimum capital is 1,000,000 DZD (private placement). Not needed for a small plant.

### Gaps
- Official CNRC nomenclature text (decree on the nomenclature des activités économiques soumises à inscription) was not read. Code numbers come from an aggregator (UpGrowth).
- Current exact CNRC fee schedule and whether NIF/NIS are issued automatically could not be verified on cnrc.dz.

## 2. Startup support schemes and financing

### Takeaway
Law 22-18 (AAPI) gives customs duty exemption, VAT franchise and IBS exemption for eligible manufacturing investments. ANADE funds micro-projects up to 10 MDZD on a triangular basis. The Startup label offers 4-year tax exemptions plus VAT-free equipment at 5% duty, but it targets innovative firms, and a conventional injection shop is unlikely to qualify.

### Cited Findings
- Law 22-18 (24 July 2022), realisation phase: customs duty exemption on imported goods/services directly used in the investment, and VAT franchise on imported or locally acquired goods/services. Operating phase: IBS exemption (and TAP, now abolished). Benefits last 3–5 years under the "secteurs" regime and 5–10 years under the "zones" and "structurants" regimes. The realisation deadline is 3 years (secteurs) or 5 years (zones/structurants) from registration. VERIFIED-snippet — [Algérie360: 5 points clés](https://www.algerie360.com/nouvelle-loi-sur-linvestissement-en-algerie-5-points-cles-a-retenir/); [ZoomAlgérie: AAPI avantages](https://zoomalgerie.com/aapi-avantages-fiscaux-douaniers-investir-algerie-etranger/); [FAOLEX text Alg190581](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/Alg190581.pdf)
- About 160 production and service activities are excluded from Law 22-18 benefits (negative list set by implementing decree). VERIFIED-snippet — [Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/14893). Whether plastic injection is on this list was NOT checked.
- ANADE (ex-ANSEJ): financing up to 10 MDZD (2025 extension). Triangular financing for young unemployed people and students: 70% bank credit, 25% ANADE interest-free loan, 5% personal contribution. VERIFIED-snippet — [UpGrowth aide création](https://www.upgrowth.dz/aide-creation-entreprise-algerie); [Univ. Sétif "Entreprendre ANADE" PDF](https://maison-entrepreneuriat.univ-setif.dz/fr/Docs/Entreprendre-ANADE.pdf); [Horizons, LF 2025 emploi](https://www.horizons.dz/2025/01/horizons-dz-dossiers-ce-que-prevoit-la-loi-de-finances-2025-pour-creer-de-lemploi/)
- Startup label: 4-year exemption from IBS, IRG, IFU and TAP (+1 year on renewal). Equipment directly used in the project is exempt from VAT and pays 5% customs duty. The Algerian Startup Fund invests about 10–100 MDZD per startup. VERIFIED-snippet (aggregator) — [UpGrowth label startup](https://www.upgrowth.dz/comment-obtenir-label-startup-algerie); [Legal Doctrine: critères](https://legal-doctrine.com/edition/Label-start-up-crit%C3%A8res-et-conditions)

### Inferences
- A Law 22-18 registration at the AAPI one-stop shop (registration certificate, list of goods) is the main way to get duty- and VAT-free import of presses, molds and auxiliaries.
- ANADE (age 18–40 historically) or ANGEM may fit a very small start (≤10 MDZD), which covers one or two used presses at most.
- Leasing (BADR, BNA, CPA, El Djazair Idjar, Ijar Leasing, MLA): ASSUMPTION that these remain available for production equipment, typically requiring 10–30% down. No 2025–26 source was found in this session.

### Gaps
- ANGEM current ceilings; ASF current ticket sizes; leasing terms and rates. Not researched due to tool-call budget.
- Whether plastics processing is on the Law 22-18 negative list. This must be checked on aapi.dz.
- ANADE tax exemption durations (historically 3 years, 6 in high plateaux/south areas — ASSUMPTION).

## 3. Importing machines, molds and raw materials

### Takeaway
Producers may import for own use, with domiciliation bancaire. Since July 2025 they must also file a semi-annual programme prévisionnel d'importation (PPI) on a Ministry of External Trade digital platform, which was renewed for 2026. Used/renovated production lines can be imported under an executive decree. The age cap has been removed for general industry and replaced by a certified remaining-life requirement, but this has changed several times. Machines (8477) pay 5% duty. Finished plastic articles (3923–3926) pay 30% duty plus a reported 60% DAPS, which strongly protects local producers.

### Cited Findings
- PPI: introduced in July 2025 for H2 2025 to reduce imports, fight fraud and promote national production. It applies to operators importing for their own account (functioning and/or equipment). It was renewed for H1 2026, with a new digital platform launched by Minister Kamel Rezig on 16 Dec 2025 covering activity codes 01 and 07. The H1 2026 filing deadline was 26 Feb 2026. VERIFIED-snippet — [TSA: PPI 2026](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/importations-lalgerie-reconduit-le-ppi-pour-2026-avec-une-nouveaute/); [TSA: reconduire le PPI en 2026?](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/importations-lalgerie-va-t-elle-reconduire-le-ppi-en-2026/); [Algérie Eco](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=235950)
- Renovated/used equipment: a 2023 change replaced the term "renovated" with "used". An executive decree signed 11 May and published in the JO on 20 May removed the age caps (previously ≤5 years for agri-food, ≤2 years for pharma). It added a minimum residual life after renovation of 10–12 years for pharma and agri-food lines. Earlier versions required ≤10 years from manufacture for general production equipment. The effectiveness criterion is certified via Algerac-accredited bodies. Equipment equivalent to local production that covers the market is excluded (agricultural case). VERIFIED-snippet; **year of the May decree not confirmed (2023 or 2025)** — [TSA: mode d'emploi](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/importation-dequipements-renoves-mode-demploi/); [Algérie Eco 2021 conditions](https://www.algerie-eco.com/2021/06/05/importation-de-chaines-et-equipements-de-production-renoves-de-nouvelles-conditions-de-dedouanement-fixees/); [Team France Export: allègement](https://www.teamfrance-export.fr/infos-sectorielles/16422/16422-allegement-des-conditions-dimportation-des-lignes-de-production-doccasion); [Radio Algérie: Chelghoum](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/9647)
- HS 8477 (machines for working rubber/plastics, incl. injection 8477.10 and parts 8477.90.91): DD 5%, VAT 19%, plus "TCS" 3% and "PRCT" 2% as shown by ConformePro. VERIFIED-snippet — [ConformePro 8477](https://conformepro.dz/ar/resources/tarif-douanier/sous-position/84.77.591000/مكابس-آلات-وأجهزة-أخرى-للقولبة-أو-التشكيل-للمطاط-أو-البلاستيك); [ConformePro 8477.90.91 parts](https://conformepro.dz/ar/resources/tarif-douanier/sous-position/84.77.909100/parties-pour-machines-à-mouler-par-injection)
- HS 3923/3924/3925/3926 finished plastic articles: DD 30%, VAT 19%, TCS 3%, PRCT 2%, and DAPS reported at 60%. VERIFIED-snippet, but the snippet's summary mislabels DAPS as an "agricultural" duty. DAPS is the "droit additionnel provisoire de sauvegarde", created in 2019 at 30–200% on about 1,095 lines. Treat the 60% figure as needing confirmation — [ConformePro 3923.10](https://conformepro.dz/ar/resources/tarif-douanier/sous-position/39.23.100000/boites-caisses-casiers-et-articles-similaires); [ConformePro 3926.40.90](https://conformepro.dz/ar/resources/tarif-douanier/sous-position/39.26.409000/أشياء-زخرفية-أخرى-من-البلاستيك); [US trade.gov Algeria import tariffs](https://trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/algeria-import-tariffs)
- LF 2026 customs measures cited by press: duty cut on some inputs (semi-auto washing-machine inputs 30%→17.5%), duty on finished washing machines/freezers raised to 17.5%, 5% duty on solar-panel equipment, exemption on electrolysers. No plastics-specific measure was found. The LF 2026 is published in JO 2025 n°88. VERIFIED-snippet — [Hespress: PLF 2026 mesures clés](https://fr.hespress.com/446261-pharmaceutique-textile-energie-les-mesures-cles-du-plf-2026.html); [ConformePro JO 2025-88 LF 2026](https://conformepro.dz/resources/joradp-sgg-algerie/journal-officiel-algerien-2025-88-قانون-المالية-لسنة-2026). Note: le360.ma and lematin.ma results on "PLF 2026" concern **Morocco**, not Algeria.

### Inferences
- The tariff structure strongly favours local injection molding: 5% on machines vs 30% duty + DAPS on finished plastic parts.
- With Law 22-18 registration, presses and molds are duty- and VAT-free (ASSUMPTION that molds 8480.71 are covered as "biens entrant directement dans la réalisation").
- Polymer duty rates (HS 3901 PE, 3902 PP) and 8480.71 molds: ASSUMPTION — historically 5% for primary polymers (with exemptions for some intermediate inputs) and 5–15% for molds; not verified this session.
- Payment: ASSUMPTION — since LFC 2009 rules were relaxed, both documentary credit (LC) and remise documentaire are allowed. Free transfers for small amounts depend on Bank of Algeria rules, and domiciliation bancaire is mandatory before shipment.

### Gaps
- Exact year and number of the latest renovated-equipment decree, and whether a general age cap still applies to plastics machinery.
- DD for 3901/3902, 8480.71, 8536 (connectors/switches, possibly DAPS-listed). The current DAPS list (last revisions ~2023–24) was not obtained.
- Meaning of "TCS 3%" / "PRCT 2%" on ConformePro. PRCT is likely the 2% solidarity contribution on imports. Whether TCS is still applied must be confirmed with douane.gov.dz.
- ALGEX role and the importer "registre"/fichier national des importateurs in 2026.

## 4. Taxes and social charges

### Takeaway
IBS is 19% for goods production (vs 23% BTPH/tourism and 26% other activities). VAT is 19% standard / 9% reduced. TAP was abolished by LF 2024. Small firms can be under the IFU (5% of turnover for production/sales, below 8 MDZD under the current regime; one source cites a 30 MDZD old ceiling). Employer social charges are about 26% of gross salary.

### Cited Findings
- IBS: 19% production of goods; 26% other activities. The BTPH/tourism rate is reported as 12% in an old source and 23% in more recent ones; 23% is current. VERIFIED-snippet — [ZoomAlgérie fiscalité entreprise](https://zoomalgerie.com/fiscalite-entreprise-algerie-ibs-tva-dividendes-obligations/); [Ecovis Tax Guide Algeria](https://global.ecovis.com/?p=10460)
- TAP abolished by LF 2024 (articles 217–231 CID repealed). VERIFIED-snippet — [KPMG Algérie LF 2024](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/dz/pdf/newsletter/loi-de-finances-2024-fr.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf); [Algérie Eco LF 2024](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=184817)
- LF 2026: extended the 9% reduced VAT to some operations (real-estate rehabilitation, health services). It clarifies refunds of excess IBS/IRG instalments and proposed an IRG cut for shareholders. VERIFIED-snippet — [Deloitte Avocats: LF 2026](https://blog.avocats.deloitte.fr/algerie-les-principales-mesures-de-la-loi-de-finances-pour-2026/?amp=1); [TSA: IRG actionnaires](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/entreprises-lalgerie-envisage-de-baisser-lirg-pour-les-actionnaires/)
- IFU: 5% of turnover for production and sale of goods. One source cites a 30 MDZD ceiling. VERIFIED-snippet, **likely outdated** — [ZoomAlgérie](https://zoomalgerie.com/?p=12826). ASSUMPTION: since LF 2020/2022 the IFU is limited to personnes physiques with a ceiling of 8 MDZD (production/sales 5%, services 12%), and companies (SARL/EURL) fall under the réel regime. Verify on mfdgi.gov.dz.
- Social security (CNAS): employer 25% + 0.5% social-works fund (≈26%), employee 9%, uncapped on full gross. Employers must register before the first hire. VERIFIED-snippet — [Mercans payroll dossier Algeria](https://dsr.mercans.com/payroll-dossiers/algeria/); [UpGrowth CNAS](https://www.upgrowth.dz/en/cnas-algeria)
- From 2026, salaries under 30,000 DZD are no longer subject to IRG. VERIFIED-snippet — [TSA: SNMG 24,000](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/algerie-le-salaire-minimum-passe-a-24-000-da-effet-retroactif-des-le-1er-janvier/)

### Inferences
- A SARL injection plant is on the réel regime: 19% IBS, 19% VAT on most sales (9% for some products), no TAP, and about 26% employer charges.
- During Law 22-18 benefit years, IBS would be 0%.

### Gaps
- Exact 2026 IFU ceiling and whether legal entities can opt for it; the minimum IBS. The full Deloitte LF 2026 memo could not be read (blocked).

## 5. Industrial land / workshops

### Takeaway
State industrial land is now handled by a dedicated agency: the Agence nationale du foncier industriel, which took over DIVINDUS Zones Industrielles assets in March 2025. ANIREF also auctions assets. Rental hangars exist on Ouedkniss (e.g. Sétif), but no published per-m² rent figure was retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- On 19 March 2025 the CPE approved the transfer of the industrial-zone land assets and obligations of EPE DIVINDUS Zones Industrielles to the Agence nationale du foncier industriel. The agency develops, connects and manages industrial zones, activity zones and technology parks on private state land. ## 6. Electricity (Sonelgaz)

### Takeaway
No verified 2025–26 industrial tariff (DA/kWh) was found. A new fixed per-quarter duty on electricity/gas bills applies from January 2025 (LF 2025, JO n°84 of 26 Dec 2024).

### Cited Findings
- LF 2025 created a fixed duty per subscriber, scaled to quarterly consumption, effective January 2025 (JO n°84, 26 Dec 2024). VERIFIED-snippet — [Algérie360](https://www.algerie360.com/vos-factures-sonelgaz-augmenteront-en-2025-nous-vous-revelons-les-montants-du-nouvel-impot/)
- Sonelgaz management has publicly argued tariff increases are "inevitable" (older statements). VERIFIED-snippet, not a current tariff — [Algérie360: Bouterfa](https://www.algerie360.com/electricite-m-bouterfa-l-augmentation-des-tarifs-est-inevitable/)

### Inferences (ASSUMPTION)
- A hydraulic 80–120 t press is typically about 15–30 kW installed (servo models lower average draw). With a dryer, chiller, mold temperature controller, granulator and compressor, 2–3 presses need roughly 100–200 kVA. That is near or above the practical low-voltage limit, so an MV (HTA, 10/30 kV) supply with a client-owned transformer (160–400 kVA) is often required.
- Algerian MV tariffs (CREG-set, frozen since ~2016) have historically been among the lowest in the region, around 4–5 DA/kWh in time-of-use bands. Treat as unverified.

### Gaps
- CREG/Sonelgaz 2025–26 tariff grid for BT/MT, connection costs and lead times. Not found (official sites not reached).

## 7. Environmental classification

### Takeaway
Classified installations are governed by Law 03-10, Décret exécutif 06-198 (regulations for classified establishments) and Décret exécutif 07-144 (nomenclature, JO n°34 of 22 May 2007). Plastics processing appears in the nomenclature, and the regime (declaration to the APC / authorisation by wali or minister) depends on capacity. The exact rubrique and thresholds for injection molding were not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Décret exécutif 07-144 of 19 May 2007 sets the nomenclature of classified installations, applying art. 23 of Law 03-10 (19 July 2003). Published in JO n°34 of 22 May 2007. VERIFIED-snippet — [UNEP LEAP / FAOLEX 07-144](https://leap.unep.org/en/countries/dz/national-legislation/decret-executif-no-07-144-du-2-joumada-el-oula-1428-correspondant)

### Inferences (ASSUMPTION)
- A small injection shop will likely fall under the lowest regimes: declaration to the président d'APC, or authorisation by the wali with a notice/study d'impact simplified depending on throughput. Expect an étude de danger only if large solvent/fuel storage exists.
- Scrap/regrind is recycled in-house. Non-recyclable waste falls under Law 01-19 on waste management.

### Gaps
- The exact 07-144 rubrique number and tonnage/kW thresholds for "transformation de matières plastiques" (need the JO text).

## 8. Product regulation: food contact, electrical, construction, automotive

### Takeaway
Food-contact plastics are regulated by an interministerial arrêté of 31 October 2021 (applying Executive Decree 16-299 of 23 Nov 2016). It sets definitions, migration limits and authorised monomers/additives (6 annexes), similar to EU 10/2011. It applies to both imports and local production.

### Cited Findings
- Arrêté interministériel of 31 Oct 2021 (24 Rabie El Aouel 1443) sets specifications for plastic materials and objects intended for food contact, under Executive Decree 16-299 (23 Nov 2016). It covers definitions, migration limits and authorised substances/additives, with 6 annexes (monomers, starting substances, etc.). It applies to mono- and multi-layer all-plastic objects, and to both imported and locally made products. VERIFIED-snippet — [FAOLEX alg210018](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/alg210018.pdf); [Ministry of Commerce avis aux opérateurs](https://commerce.gov.dz/fr/avis/avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-17)

### Inferences (ASSUMPTION)
- Food-contact producers will need migration test reports (from an accredited lab such as CACQE or a foreign lab) and supplier declarations of compliance for resin and masterbatch.
- Electrical accessories (HS 8536: sockets, switches) fall under mandatory technical regulations and IANOR NA standards aligned to IEC 60669 / 60884. Conformity is checked at import and the market.
- Automotive (Stellantis Fiat Oran, possibly Renault Oran): tier-1/2 suppliers are typically expected to be ISO 9001 certified and on an IATF 16949 roadmap, with PPAP/APQP. No Algeria-specific source was found.

### Gaps
- IANOR standard numbers, whether certification marks are mandatory, construction-product conformity rules, and automotive supplier requirements. Not researched within budget.

## 9. Labor: minimum wage and typical wages

### Takeaway
The SNMG rose from 20,000 to 24,000 DZD/month (gross) effective 1 January 2026, announced at the Council of Ministers on 30 Nov 2025. Salaries under 30,000 DZD are IRG-exempt. No reliable wage data for injection operators or setters was found.

### Cited Findings
