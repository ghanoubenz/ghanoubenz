## 1. Is any PP or HDPE produced in Algeria in 2026? (Sonatrach / STEP / Skikda)

### Takeaway
No PP is produced in Algeria in 2026. The Arzew PDH-PP complex (550 kt/yr) has **stalled**: TotalEnergies withdrew, and the Petrofac-led EPC consortium stopped work. No restart date has been announced, and the original end-2026 delivery is now "out of reach". Skikda CP2K historically made HDPE (Polymed, 130 kt/yr, Phillips process), but I found no evidence that it produced commercial HDPE in 2025–2026. A startup should plan for **100% imported PP and HDPE**.

## 2. Which resins are imported, and from where?

### Takeaway
Essentially all thermoplastics used by Algerian converters are imported: PP, HDPE, LDPE, LLDPE, ABS, PA6/66, POM, PC, PET, TPE/TPU, most masterbatch and additives. PVC is likely imported as well, since CP1K is not active. Typical origins are Gulf producers (SABIC, Borouge, Sipchem, Tasnee), Turkey, Europe and Asia. Trade-database figures were paywalled.

### Cited Findings

## 5. Practical buying process (packaging, MOQ, payment, documents)

### Takeaway
The process could not be confirmed from Algerian sources. The standard global practice below is labelled as an estimate and should be confirmed with the distributors in §3.

### Cited Findings
- No Algeria-specific source was retrieved on packaging, MOQ or payment terms. NOT FOUND.

### Inferences (ESTIMATE, from standard industry practice)
- Packaging: 25 kg PE valve or FFS bags; 55 bags (1,375 kg) or 40 bags (1 t) per pallet; octabins (about 1 t) for big users; 20 ft container about 16–24 t.
- Lot sizes for a startup: 100 kg = 4 bags (distributor stock, premium price); 500 kg = 20 bags; 1 t = 40 bags (about one pallet). Importing directly makes sense only from about one container upward.
- Payment: proforma, then bank transfer (virement) or cheque, often cash on pickup. Credit only for established customers. Since Algerian law has required non-cash payment above set thresholds, bank transfer or cheque is safer for an invoiced purchase.
- A proper invoice (facture) with VAT needs the buyer's registre de commerce (RC), NIF, NIS and article d'imposition. Without them you get only a bon or no invoice, and cannot recover VAT.
- Documents to request: TDS, SDS (FDS), CoA per lot, lot or batch number printed on the bag, and the food-contact compliance statement (EU 10/2011 / FDA 21 CFR 177.1520) if relevant.
- Transport: pickup from the distributor's warehouse, or a hired truck. Bags must be kept dry and out of UV light.
- Ouedkniss: small-lot resale ("matière première", regrind, broyé) is believed common, but this is not verified here. Insist on original sealed bags and a TDS.

## 6. Import rules (HS 3901/3902, VAT, DAPS, import programme)

### Takeaway
PP copolymer (3902.30.90.00) carries 5% customs duty, 19% VAT, 3% TCS and a 2% PRCT minimum. Primary-form polymers are inputs and are not believed to be on the DAPS list, though this was not verified. Direct import needs an importer-status company and is governed by the 2023+ import pre-programming framework.

### Cited Findings
- HS 3902.30.90.00 (propylene copolymers, other forms), Algeria: DD 5%, TVA 19%, TCS 3%, PRCT minimum 2%. Exemptions may apply under bilateral or regional conventions (Algeria–Jordan, Algeria–Tunisia and others). VERIFIED (snippet) — [ConformePro tariff](https://conformepro.dz/ar/resources/tarif-douanier/sous-position/39.02.309000/copolymères-de-propylene-sous-autres-formes)
- DAPS (droit additionnel provisoire de sauvegarde) took effect on 2019-01-30, replacing the 2018 import-suspension list. It covers 1,095 tariff lines at mostly 60–70% (maximum 200%). VERIFIED (snippet) — [Spain Barreras Comerciales](https://barrerascomerciales.comercio.gob.es/en-us/noticias/Paginas/ARGELIA-aranceles-sustituiran-a-las-prohibiciones-de-importacion.aspx)
- HS 3902 covers polymers of propylene and other olefins in primary forms (3902.10 PP, 3902.30 copolymers). — [WCO](https://www.wcotradetools.org/en/harmonized-system/2022/fr/073902)

### Inferences
- ESTIMATE: 3902.10 (PP homopolymer) and 3901.20 (HDPE ≥ 0.94) very likely carry the same 5% DD as other industrial raw materials. Verify on the douane.gov.dz tariff.
- ESTIMATE: GAFTA origin (Saudi, UAE, Egypt) can give 0% duty with an EUR.1/GAFTA certificate of origin. EU origin may receive Association Agreement reductions.
- ESTIMATE: Since 2023–2024, importers must file an import pre-programme (programme prévisionnel d'importation) with the Ministry of Commerce/ALGEX and get bank domiciliation. Importing for resale requires an import-trade RC, while a manufacturer may import inputs for its own use under its production RC. A small startup importing 1 t is impractical. Buy locally until consumption reaches container scale.

## 8. Technical explainer: reading a resin TDS (practical)

### Takeaway
"PP" alone is not a specification. Specify **polymer type and sub-type, MFR, density, additive package, regulatory status and supplier grade name**, and get a TDS, SDS and per-lot CoA. (General engineering knowledge; ESTIMATE / standard practice; supporting source [INRS PP sheet](https://inrs.fr/dam/jcr:eb0af816-589f-424f-87b3-1f0f938ed452/POLYMERE_PP.pdf).)

### Cited Findings
- The Skikda HDPE process controls melt index and density through ethylene concentration, temperature, catalyst productivity, and hydrogen and hexene. This shows that MFI and density are designed-in grade properties, not incidental ones. — [Univ. Constantine 3 thesis](https://dspace.univ-constantine3.dz/jspui/bitstream/123456789/5037/1/281Etude%20thermique%20du%20reacteur%20et%20rechauffeur%20d%27eau%20pour%20la%20production%20%20de%20polythylene%20a%20haute%20densite%20cp2k%20skikda.pdf)

### Inferences (practical explainer — general knowledge)
- **MFR/MFI** (g/10 min): ISO 1133 / ASTM D1238. PP is measured at 230 °C / 2.16 kg and PE at 190 °C / 2.16 kg. Check the conditions before comparing. Higher MFR means lower molecular weight, easier flow, thinner walls and faster cycles, but lower impact strength and ESCR. **Injection grades are usually 8–50 g/10 min** (thin-wall packaging 50–100+). **Blow molding, pipe, film and extrusion grades are low (about 0.1–2)** because they need melt strength. A low-MFR extrusion grade bought as "PP" will short-shot, need high pressure and cause warpage.
- **Density**: HDPE is 0.941–0.965 g/cm³, where higher means stiffer but more stress-crack prone. PP is about 0.90–0.91.
- **Shrinkage**: PP about 1.0–2.5% and HDPE about 1.5–3%, both higher than ABS (0.4–0.7%). Mold dimensions are cut for a specific resin, so changing the grade or family changes part dimensions.
- **PP types**: *homopolymer* is stiffest with the best heat resistance, but brittle when cold. *Random copolymer* (ethylene in the chain) is clearer and more flexible, used for transparent housewares and food containers. *Impact (block/heterophasic) copolymer* has a rubber phase and gives good impact strength at low temperature, used for crates, buckets and automotive parts.
- **Additives**: nucleating or clarifying agents (faster cycle, clarity), antistatic, slip, and UV stabilization (HALS). UV stabilization is essential for outdoor parts, which crack within months without it in Algerian sunlight. Masterbatch can add UV protection if the base grade lacks it.
- **Food contact**: request a declaration of compliance to EU Regulation 10/2011 and/or FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 for the exact grade. Algerian rules generally follow these references.
- **What to request with every lot**: TDS (MFR, density, flexural modulus, impact, HDT), SDS, CoA with lot number matching the bag, regulatory statement (food, RoHS/REACH), and the producer's grade name. Examples of the form: SABIC PP 575P homopolymer (MFR about 11), or a random-copolymer grade. Ask "what is the grade code?" and not just "do you have PP?".
