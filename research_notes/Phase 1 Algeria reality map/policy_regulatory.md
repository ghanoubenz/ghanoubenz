## 1. Import ban from 1 Jan 2027 on automotive plastic parts that GPI Tissemsilt can make

### Takeaway
The cutoff is real but has no legal text yet. On 24 Sep 2026 PM Sifi Ghrieb publicly set **31 Dec 2026** as the end of imports of automotive plastic parts "that can be manufactured by" the state plant GPI (General Plastic Injection, Tissemsilt). From early 2027, licensed car makers and assemblers must source those parts locally. A **cahier des charges** (not yet published) is to define supply conditions between GPI and the OEMs. **No list of parts and no JORA text, decree or arrêté was found.**

### Cited Findings
- **OFFICIAL (announcement):** The PM set 31 Dec 2026 as the end date for imports of plastic parts that GPI can manufacture. He was quoted: "No plastic part entering the automotive industry that could be manufactured by GPI will be imported from the beginning of next year." Manufacturers and approved operators must source plastic parts and components locally from early 2027. The measure "will be governed by a cahier des charges defining supply conditions between the company and the manufacturers". Reported by state radio and multiple outlets on 24–25 Sep 2026 — [Radio Algérie FR, node 95081](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/95081); [allAfrica/APS relay, 24 Sep 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202609240635.html); [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/automobile-lalgerie-nimportera-plus-de-pieces-plastiques-des-2027/); [Algérie Eco, 24 Sep 2026](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/09/24/automobile-les-pieces-plastiques-fabriquees-en-algerie-ne-seront-plus-importees-des-2027/); [Algérie360](https://www.algerie360.com/sifi-ghrieb-importation-pieces-plastiques-automobile-tissemsilt/); [El Khabar FR](https://www.elkhabar.com/fr/nation/je-privilegierais-un-titre-plus-direct-centre-sur-la-decision-annoncee-industrie-automobile-sifi-ghrieb-annonce-l-interdiction-d-importer-certaines-pieces-plastiques-264970); [Observ'Algérie, 25 Sep 2026](https://observalgerie.com/2026/09/25/economie/lalgerie-annonce-la-fin-de-limportation-des-pieces-plastiques-pour-voitures). **STRONG SIGNAL** (official announcement; no legal instrument).
- **Date conflict:** one search summary placed the announcement on "2 September 2026". All dated articles are 24–25 Sep 2026, and the Ghrieb visit/inauguration is reported as 24 Sep. I use 24 Sep 2026 — same sources as above.
- The PM also asked that the molds needed for the various automotive plastic components be at the factory **before year-end** — [search synthesis of Radio Algérie EN node 85004 / TSA](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/en/node/85004). **STRONG SIGNAL.** This implies GPI does not yet hold most of its molds.
- **GPI profile (PRESS):** GPI belongs to the public holding ACS (Algeria Chemical Specialities). It is located in Khemisti, Sidi Mansour zone, Tissemsilt, and was rebuilt on the assets of the former Plastic Algeria Components (PAC), which were transferred to ACS. The project was launched by the PM around 1 May 2026, with production scheduled for Sep 2026. Reported capacity is "251,000 parts/year", sized to equip about 200,000 vehicles, with an initial integration rate of 40% rising to 70% — [Dzair Tube, 1 May 2026](https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-general-plastic-injection-unit-in-tissemsilt-to-deepen-auto-integration-drive/); [Dzair Tube, 3 May 2026](https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-to-launch-plastic-auto-components-plant-in-tissemsilt-in-september/); [Managers.tn, 5 May 2026](https://managers.tn/2026/05/05/algerie-lancement-dune-usine-de-pieces-plastiques-automobiles-pour-reduire-la-dependance-aux-importations/); [TSA: usine pour 200 000 véhicules](https://tsa-algerie.com/automobile-lalgerie-lance-une-usine-de-composants-plastiques-pour-200-000-vehicules/); [Ecofin, 27 Sep 2026](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-industry/2709-59266-algeria-s-acs-opens-plastics-plant-to-supply-local-automakers-and-cut-imports). **STRONG SIGNAL** on existence, timing and ownership. **WEAK SIGNAL** on the capacity figure; see Inferences.
- No published list of covered parts (bumpers, dashboards, trims, etc.) was found in any source — same sources as above. **UNKNOWN.**

### Inferences
- **Legal status:** a PM instruction, without a JORA instrument, is enforceable in practice through the OEM cahier des charges, customs/PPI visas (the PPI platform can refuse import lines) and the "programme" approvals that car makers need. Expect enforcement through the PPI and OEM licence conditions rather than a tariff line ban. ASSUMPTION.
- **"251,000 parts/year for 200,000 vehicles"** is about 1.25 parts per car. That only makes sense if it means part *sets* or a narrow range (e.g. bumpers). The figure is probably garbled in the press, so do not use it to size the remaining market.
- **Effect on a small 100–120 t molder:**
  - **Threat:** big exterior and interior parts (bumpers, fascias, dashboards) need 500–2,500 t presses, which a 120 t shop cannot make anyway. The reserved scope therefore overlaps little with a 120 t shop.
  - **Opportunity:** small clips, fasteners, caps, grommets, connectors housings and small brackets (≤120 t parts) may (a) fall outside GPI's real capability, yet (b) face the same political pressure to localise. OEMs (Stellantis Oran and others) will need local sources for small parts to prove integration. GPI itself may sub-contract small parts it cannot run economically on large presses. **WEAK SIGNAL / ASSUMPTION.**
  - **Precedent:** the state has shown it will close imports by fiat once one local plant claims capacity. A private molder who demonstrates capacity on a part family could lobby (via the Ministry of Industry / sous-traitance exchange) for the same protection. This is double-edged: a competitor with state backing can do the same to you.

### Gaps
- Cahier des charges text, the part list, and whether it covers after-sales spare parts or only OEM assembly lines. Watch JORA and industrie.gov.dz in Q4 2026.
- GPI press tonnage range and mold ownership/sourcing (who makes GPI's molds: imported, or local tenders?).

### Takeaway
OEM integration commitments are the demand engine for local plastic parts. Press reports Stellantis committing to **30% integration in 2026**, with the cahier des charges requiring **40% after 5 years** of activity. Stellantis is actively contracting local subcontractors (Mechanica Oran, Feb 2026). The governing official text was not read this session.

### Cited Findings

PRESS. **STRONG SIGNAL.**
- Fiat Algérie invited local parts makers to Mechanica Algeria, Oran, 10–12 Feb 2026. Stellantis signed five new contracts with Algerian subcontractors there. The Oran plant moved to CKD production of the Grande Panda. Stellantis works with Italy's Sigit in synergy with ENPC/Siplast on local plastic parts — [Motorsactu](https://motorsactu.com/fiat-algerie-appel-aux-fabricants-de-pieces-locales/); [TSA: Stellantis cinq sous-traitants](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/automobile-stellantis-etoffe-son-reseau-avec-cinq-sous-traitants-algeriens/); [Algérie360: nouveaux fournisseurs locaux](https://www.algerie360.com/fiat-algerie-accelere-lintegration-industrielle-avec-de-nouveaux-fournisseurs-locaux/). **STRONG SIGNAL** (company/press).

### Inferences
- The legal base is an executive decree setting the cahier des charges for vehicle manufacturing (ASSUMPTION: DE 22-383 of Nov 2022, from training knowledge; not verified this session).
- Integration-rate pressure plus the GPI cutoff means OEM purchasing teams are *mandated* to find local plastic suppliers in 2026–27. This is the strongest demand-side policy tailwind found. IATF/PPAP expectations remain a barrier for a start-up.

### Gaps

## 3. Used / renovated machinery imports: which decree is in force?

### Takeaway
**Executive Decree 24-241 of 22 July 2024 (JORA no. 50) is the text in force.** Used production lines and equipment may be cleared only if **≤5 years** from manufacture or commissioning (agricultural equipment ≤7 years). The "later decree removing age caps" reported in earlier notes is actually **an earlier decree**: DE 21-200 of 11 May 2021, amending DE 20-312 of 15 Nov 2020. That regime was **repealed** by 24-241. No 2025–2026 amendment of 24-241 was found.

### Cited Findings
- **OFFICIAL:** DE 24-241 of 16 Moharram 1446 / 22 Jul 2024, JORA no. 50, sets "modalités de dédouanement, pour la mise à la consommation, des chaînes et équipements de production ainsi que des équipements et matériels agricoles, utilisés". It permits clearance of used lines/equipment whose manufacture or commissioning date does not exceed **5 years**, and agricultural equipment ≤7 years. Further conditions:
  - a conformity/qualification certificate is required from the wilaya director in charge of industry;
  - the applicant must be registered at the CNRC;
  - the equipment must be put into service for the operator's own needs within **6 months** of clearance.
  - The decree **repeals DE 20-312 of 15 Nov 2020**, as amended.
  Sources: [douane.gov.dz PDF of JORA 50](https://douane.gov.dz/IMG/pdf/decret_executif_no_24-241_du_22_07_2024_fr-2.pdf); [industrie.gov.dz: chaînes et équipements utilisés](https://www.industrie.gov.dz/fr/chaines-et-equipements-utilises-fr/); [Maghreb Émergent AR](https://maghrebemergent.news/ar/استيراد-الترخيص-بجمركة-العتاد-الفلاح/); [Algérie Direct AR: "مصانع أقل من 5 سنوات"](https://www.algeriedirect.dz/%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%85-%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B0%D9%8A-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%B9-%D8%A3%D9%82/); [Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/49362). **VERIFIED** (official instrument; content consistent across snippets of the JORA PDF and the press).
- **History, to resolve the conflict.** DE 20-312 (15 Nov 2020) set the "renovated lines" regime. DE 21-200 (11 May 2021) **removed** its age caps for agri-food (5 y) and pharma (2 y), replacing them with a minimum residual life after renovation of 10 y (12 y for pharma/agri-food). It kept a general ceiling of ≤10 years from manufacture. A 2022 government decision further eased access ("feu vert", May 2022) — [Algérie Eco, 5 Jun 2021](https://algerie-eco.com/2021/06/05/importation-de-chaines-et-equipements-de-production-renoves-de-nouvelles-conditions-de-dedouanement-fixees/); [Team France Export](https://www.teamfrance-export.fr/infos-sectorielles/16422/16422-allegement-des-conditions-dimportation-des-lignes-de-production-doccasion); [APS](https://www.aps.dz/economie/124066-industrie-allegement-des-conditions-d-acquisition-de-chaines-et-d-equipements-de-production-renoves); [La Nouvelle République, 17 May 2022](https://www.lnr-dz.com/2022/05/17/le-gouvernement-donne-le-feu-vert-a-limportation-des-chaines-de-production-renovees/); [Echorouk EN](https://www.echoroukonline.com/new-conditions-for-importing-production-equipment-and-end-of-concessions-era). All these predate 24-241, which repealed 20-312 "as amended", and with it 21-200. **VERIFIED** (chronology).
- A targeted search for any decree amending 24-241 in 2025–2026 (FR and AR) returned nothing — searches run 2026-10-04. **UNKNOWN → treat as not amended**, but the consolidated JORA was not checked.

### Inferences
- A used press must be ≤5 years old (from manufacture or first commissioning) to be cleared, with a wilaya industry-directorate certificate. Most "cheap used" European presses (10–20 years old) are **not importable**. A 2021–2022 build bought in 2026 is borderline. In practice, **a new Chinese servo press is the realistic route**, which is also what AAPI benefits favour.
- Earlier note `machines_auxiliaries_landed_cost.md` §5 is correct. Note `regulatory_legal_tax.md` §3 (age caps removed, "11 May / 20 May" decree) describes the **2021** regime and is **superseded**.

### Gaps
- Whether 24-241's certificate applies per single machine or only per "chaîne" (the text speaks of "chaînes et équipements", so a single machine is very likely covered — ASSUMPTION).
- Whether used equipment can benefit from Law 22-18 AAPI exemptions.

## 4. Duty rates: molds (8480.71), resin (3901.20 / 3902.10), finished plastic articles and DAPS

### Takeaway
Molds 8480.71 show **DD 5%** + VAT 19% + "TCS" 3% + "PRCT" 2% on ConformePro. PP copolymer (3902.30) is **5%** (earlier note). Rates for 3901.20 and 3902.10 specifically were **not seen**; 5% is the likely band (ASSUMPTION). Finished plastic articles carry **30% DD**, and at least 3923.10 and 3926.40.90 also carry **DAPS 60%** (earlier note). DAPS on 3924, 3925 and 3917 was **not confirmed**.

### Cited Findings
- HS 8480.71.00.00 "Moules pour le caoutchouc ou les matières plastiques, pour le moulage par injection ou par compression": DD 5%, TVA 19%, TCS 3%, PRCT 2% — [ConformePro 84.80.710000](https://conformepro.dz/resources/tarif-douanier/sous-position/84.80.710000/قوالب-للمطاط-أو-المواد-البلاستيكية-للقولبة-بالحقن-أو-بالضغط). **STRONG SIGNAL** (private aggregator of the DGD tariff, read via search summary; douane.gov.dz not reachable).
- HS 3902.30.90.00 PP copolymer: DD 5%, TVA 19%, TCS 3%, PRCT 2% — [ConformePro 39.02.309000](https://conformepro.dz/ar/resources/tarif-douanier/sous-position/39.02.309000/copolymères-de-propylene-sous-autres-formes) (from earlier note `resin_supply.md`). **STRONG SIGNAL.**
- HS 3901.20.30.00 (PE density ≥0.94, granules) exists as a national sub-line on ConformePro, but the rate was not visible in the snippet — [ConformePro 39.01.203000](https://conformepro.dz/resources/tarif-douanier/sous-position/39.01.203000/polyethylene-dune-densite-egale-ou-superieure-a-094-sous-forme-de-granules). **UNKNOWN** (likely 5%, ASSUMPTION). 3902.10 (PP homopolymer): **UNKNOWN**.
- **DAPS legal base (OFFICIAL):** Executive Decree 18-230 of 25 Sep 2018 sets the procedure. A Ministry of Commerce arrêté of 26 Jan 2019 (in force 27 Jan 2019) lists 1,095 tariff lines at 30–200% — [Classe Export](https://classe-export.com/index.php/actus/24860-algerie-mise-place-dun-droit-additionnel-provisoire-de-sauvegarde-daps/); [FreshPlaza](https://www.freshplaza.fr/article/9068500/algerie-1095-produits-sont-soumis-au-daps/); [CCI Mezghena list](https://cci-mezghena.dz/actualite/liste-des-marchandises-soumises-au-droit-additionnel-provisoire-de-sauvegarde-daps). **VERIFIED** (instruments). The list has been revised since; the current version was not obtained.
- DAPS 60% shown on 3923.10 (boxes, crates) and 3926.40.90 (decorative articles), with DD 30% — [ConformePro 3923.10](https://conformepro.dz/ar/resources/tarif-douanier/sous-position/39.23.100000/boites-caisses-casiers-et-articles-similaires); [ConformePro 3926.40.90](https://conformepro.dz/ar/resources/tarif-douanier/sous-position/39.26.409000/أشياء-زخرفية-أخرى-من-البلاستيك) (earlier note). **STRONG SIGNAL** for those two lines only.
- 3924, 3925, 3917 and the 3926.90 "autres" lines (where most technical B2B components fall): **UNKNOWN**. The search budget was exhausted before checking.

### Inferences
- **Tariff escalation favours a local molder:** inputs are 5% (molds, resin, machines 8477), while finished plastic articles are 30% plus DAPS on some lines. Effective protection on a molded part is roughly 25 points of duty, plus DAPS where it applies, plus the import friction of the PPI and domiciliation (§5–6).
- **Caveat:** technical components imported *as parts of machines/vehicles* are usually classified in chapters 84/85/87 (parts headings), not 3926, often at 5–15% with no DAPS. Protection for B2B components is therefore weaker than the 3923/3926 figures suggest. ASSUMPTION; check line by line for each target product.
- "PRCT 2%" is most likely the 2% solidarity contribution on imports (LF 2018). The meaning of "TCS 3%" is still unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- An official DGD (douane.gov.dz) tariff readout for 3901.20, 3902.10, 8480.71, and 3926.90.xx with DAPS status. A local transitaire can confirm this in one phone call.

## 5. Bank of Algeria Note 01/DGC/2026 (14 May 2026) and related 2026 banking rules

### Takeaway
All goods imports must be domiciled **before shipment**. A **120% provision**, in deposits and/or allocated credit lines, must be constituted at domiciliation, **at least 30 days before shipment**. Transport documents dated before domiciliation lead to refusal, save "duly justified, expressly authorised" exceptions. **No source found addresses advance payments/deposits to suppliers, services, or a de minimis amount.** Those points are **UNKNOWN**. A separate Instruction 05-2026 (19 May 2026) caps the outstanding imports of **resale-as-is importers** at 100% of equity; it does not appear to target producers.

### Cited Findings
- **OFFICIAL:** Note no. 01/DGC/2026 of 14 May 2026, addressed to approved intermediary banks:
  - every goods import must be domiciled before any shipment by the foreign supplier;
  - no domiciliation may be accepted after effective shipment, except as expressly authorised by the regulations;
  - banks must refuse if the transport document predates domiciliation;
  - a 120% financial provision is constituted at domiciliation, at least 30 days before shipment, as deposits and/or by allocation to credit lines;
  - stated aims are fighting fraud and irregular capital transfers, prior monitoring of import commitments, and statistical and prudential control.
  Sources: [Banque d'Algérie, notes 2026](https://www.bank-of-algeria.dz/notes-2026/); [BoA PDF](https://www.bank-of-algeria.dz/stoodroa/2026/05/note-01-DGC-2026.pdf); [Legal Doctrine](https://legal-doctrine.com/en/edition/banque-dalgerie-une-nouvelle-obligation-de-domiciliation-bancaire-prealable-pour-les-importations-ddc95e1ebdfdfe38f59f0448921114d6); [Algérie Eco, 14 May 2026](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/05/14/importations-la-banque-dalgerie-impose-la-domiciliation-bancaire-avant-lexpedition-des-marchandises/); [BNP Paribas El Djazaïr client notice](https://www.bnpparibas.dz/obligation-de-domiciliation-bancaire-prealable-a-toute-expedition/); [Maghreb Émergent](https://maghrebemergent.news/fr/domiciliation-bancaire-la-banque-dalgerie-impose-de-nouvelles-regles-aux-importateurs/). **VERIFIED** (official note; consistent snippets incl. a bank's client notice).
- **PRESS:** operators criticised the note — [Maghreb Émergent: "ne fait pas l'unanimité"](https://maghrebemergent.news/fr/domiciliation-bancaire-prealable-la-note-de-la-banque-dalgerie-qui-ne-fait-pas-lunanimite/); [Cap Algérie, 20 May 2026: "resserre méthodiquement l'étau"](https://capalgerie.dz/index.php/2026/05/20/importations-la-banque-dalgerie-resserre-methodiquement-letau/). No relaxation was found through early Oct 2026. **STRONG SIGNAL** (absence of relaxation, limited search).
- **OFFICIAL:** Instruction 05-2026 of 19 May 2026. For imports of goods for resale as-is, an operator's outstanding unsettled imports across all banks must never exceed 100% of its equity — [Algérie Eco, 19 May 2026](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/05/19/importations-pour-la-revente-en-letat-la-banque-dalgerie-fixe-de-nouvelles-exigences-en-matiere-de-surface-financiere-des-operateurs/); [EcoTimes DZ](https://ecotimesdz.com/instruction-05-2026-banque-algerie-regulation-importations/). **STRONG SIGNAL.**
- **OFFICIAL:** On 28 Jul 2026 the BoA required banks to domiciliate **exports** within 48 h of a complete file — [Algérie Eco, 28 Jul 2026](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/07/28/exportations-la-banque-dalgerie-fixe-un-delai-de-48-heures-aux-banques-pour-la-domiciliation-bancaire/). **STRONG SIGNAL.**
- **Services:** the ABEF (bank association) announced "two new measures" on service imports and domiciliation (title only; date and content not retrieved) — [El Watan](https://elwatan.dz/operations-dimportation-de-services-et-domiciliation-bancaire-labef-annonce-deux-nouvelles-mesures/). Earlier note: service imports need domiciliation and pay the domiciliation tax. **WEAK SIGNAL** on content.
- **Cash:** BoA note of 4 Jan 2026 (replacing note 02/DGIG/2025 of 22 Dec 2025) governs funding commercial accounts by scriptural means or cash in line with turnover, i.e. tighter cash controls — [Algérie Eco, 5 Jan 2026](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/01/05/alimentation-comptes-commerciaux-nouvelle-note-banque-dalgerie/). **STRONG SIGNAL.**

### Inferences
- **Cash-cycle impact on a start-up:** for a USD 25k press, about DZD equivalent of USD 30k (120%) must sit at the bank at least 30 days before the supplier ships. A typical Chinese 30% deposit at order plus 70% before shipment then needs bank cooperation. Whether a pre-shipment *advance* transfer is allowed under the domiciliation is **not addressed** by any snippet. Assume the bank will pay only against documents (L/C or remise documentaire) unless it agrees otherwise in writing. ASSUMPTION; ask the domiciliating bank.
- Lead time is now order → domiciliation (+PPI visa, §6) → ≥30 days → shipment → 38–45 days sea. Plan **3–4 months from PO to machine on floor**, and the same for every new imported mold.
- This friction also hits *competing importers* of finished parts. Every imported part is now slower and more cash-intensive, which strengthens the local-supply pitch (pain signal for buyers).

### Gaps
- Full text of 01/DGC/2026 (advance payments, de minimis, express shipments, samples, services).
- ABEF services measures (content and date).

## 6. PPI (programme prévisionnel d'importation): H2 2026 and 2027

### Takeaway
The PPI is a semi-annual import forecast filed on a Ministry of Foreign Trade & Export Promotion platform. It was introduced 9 Jul 2025 (retroactive to 1 Jul 2025), renewed for 2026 on a new platform launched 16 Dec 2025 for activity codes 01 (production) and 07, and extended to ANADE/NESDA micro-enterprises. The H1 2026 deadline was 26 Feb 2026 (later extended). H2 2026 filing reportedly closed **31 Jul 2026**. **Nothing published yet on 2027.**

### Cited Findings
- PPI instituted by the Ministry of Foreign Trade and Export Promotion on 9 Jul 2025, with effect from 1 Jul 2025. ## 7. AAPI incentives (Law 22-18) and the negative list

### Takeaway
DE 22-300 (8 Sep 2022) excludes about 160 activities and some goods from Law 22-18 benefits. The published excerpts name **"activités de conditionnement et d'emballage"** among excluded activities, and **recoverable packaging**, road transport, office/IT equipment among non-eligible goods. **Plastics transformation is not named** in any excerpt found. Molds and auxiliaries are not on the non-eligible goods excerpts. Whether "fabrication d'emballages en plastique" is caught by "conditionnement et emballage" is **UNKNOWN**, and this matters if the product mix includes packaging.

### Cited Findings
- **OFFICIAL:** DE 22-300 of 8 Sep 2022 fixes the lists of activities, goods and services not eligible for advantages, and the minimum thresholds for the transfer guarantee. About 160 production and service activities are excluded, e.g. tobacco, rebar, grey cement, mineral water, beverages, real-estate promotion, crafts, trade, services, **"activités de conditionnement et d'emballage"**, banking and insurance. Non-eligible goods include own-account road transport, office and communication equipment not directly used in production, **recoverable packaging**, social equipment, and IT hardware — [Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/14893); [Algérie Eco, 20 Sep 2022](https://algerie-eco.com/2022/09/20/investissement-pres-de-160-activites-de-production-et-de-services-exclues-des-avantages/); [Ministry of Industry: textes d'application](https://www.industrie.gov.dz/fr/publication-textes-dapplication-loi-investissement-joradp/); [AAPI Guide investisseur 2024](https://aapi.dz/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Guide-Investisseur_HCNRLI-1.pdf). **VERIFIED** (instrument and excerpts). Plastics not named: **UNKNOWN** (full annex not read).
- Benefits: duty exemption and VAT franchise in the realisation phase; IBS exemption 3–5 years ("secteurs") — earlier note `regulatory_legal_tax.md` §2.

### Inferences
- Technical components for industry (automotive, electrical, appliance, hydraulic) are very unlikely to be "conditionnement/emballage". Register the project under a technical-parts code (e.g. 111305 "articles en plastique NDA"), not 111310 "emballages", to avoid the risk. ASSUMPTION; confirm with the AAPI guichet unique by asking specifically about the 22-300 annex for codes 1113xx.
- At 5% duty on molds and machines, the AAPI duty saving is small (about 5% + 3% + 2%). The **VAT franchise (19%) is the big cash saving**, about USD 4–5k on a USD 25k press and similar on each mold.

### Gaps
- Full DE 22-300 annex (or amendments since 2022) as it relates to codes 1113xx; eligibility of used equipment; whether molds bought *after* the realisation phase (new products) can be added to the AAPI list.

## 8. Sous-traitance (subcontracting) exemption regime: a possible extra lever

### Takeaway
An interministerial arrêté (2020) grants **5-year exemption from customs duties and VAT** on components and raw materials, imported or local, used by an **approved subcontractor** producing assemblies and sub-assemblies for the **mechanical, electronic and electrical industries** (which includes automotive). It requires approval tied to a contracting principal and a file to the Ministry of Industry. If still active in 2026, it could make **resin duty- and VAT-free** for a molder supplying Stellantis, appliance or electrical makers.

### Cited Findings
- The conditions for duty and VAT exemption on components and raw materials imported or acquired locally by approved subcontractors were fixed in Nov 2020. The exemption covers producing assemblies and sub-assemblies for mechanical, electronic and electrical industries. It lasts 5 years from the exemption decision, is valid only for subcontracting contracts in force, and is open to subcontractors approved by an ordering producer. The file goes to the Ministry of Industry — [Algérie Eco, 22 Nov 2020](https://algerie-eco.com/2020/11/22/industrie-les-conditions-dexemption-de-taxes-sur-les-composants-de-sous-traitance-fixees/); [JORA 2020 no. 67 PDF (mirror)](https://avocatalgerien.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/F2020067.pdf); [Radio Algérie, 17 May 2020](https://radioalgerie.dz/news/fr/article/20200517/193681.html); [TSA: sous-traitance automobile/électronique](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/sous-traitance-dans-lautomobile-et-lelectronique-la-nouvelle-reglementation-entre-en-vigueur/); [Ministry of Industry: lancement du régime d'exemption au profit des sous-traitants](https://www.industrie.gov.dz/lancement-du-regime-dimportation-des-chaines-de-production-renovees-et-le-regime-dexemption-au-profit-des-sous-traitants/). **STRONG SIGNAL** that the regime exists; 2026 status **UNKNOWN**.

### Inferences
- This is the most underused lever found. A molder with a signed supply contract from an OEM or appliance maker could apply. It stacks with the AAPI exemption (equipment) to cover inputs too. Verify that it has not been repealed by later finance laws.

### Gaps
- Whether the regime is still open in 2026 and its application platform, and whether plastic raw material (resin) qualifies as "matières premières" under it (the text says components *and* raw materials, so probably yes).

## 10. Public-procurement local preference

### Takeaway
Law 23-12 (public procurement general rules, 2023) grants a **25% preference margin** to products of Algerian origin and/or to Algerian-law firms majority-owned by resident nationals, proven by a CCI certificate of Algerian origin. Its implementing regulation was reported as not yet adopted, so the PD 15-247 rules still apply. The benefit to a molder is indirect: it helps its customers who bid on public tenders with locally made assemblies.

### Cited Findings
- Law 23-12 sets a 25% preference margin for Algerian-origin products and resident-national-owned Algerian firms (art. 83 per the source). For supplies it applies to locally manufactured products on presentation of a certificate of Algerian origin from the CCI. Implementing regulation not yet adopted; previous provisions remain applicable — [CMS Law: marge de préférence nationale](https://cms.law/fr/fra/publication/flash-info-algerie-marches-publics-precisions-sur-l-application-de-la-marge-de-preference-nationale); [CMS Law EN](https://cms.law/en/fra/publication/flash-info-algeria-public-contracts-regulations-implementing-the-margin-of-preference-for-goods-and-services-of-algerian-origin). **STRONG SIGNAL** (law firm summary; the CMS note date and whether the regulation has since been adopted are unknown).

### Inferences
- Utilities and state firms (Sonelgaz, ADE water, SNTF, Cosider) buying assemblies such as meters, electrical boxes or fittings will prefer suppliers who can certify Algerian origin. Plastic parts molded locally help an assembler qualify. This is a secondary demand lever.

### Gaps
- Origin rules (value-added threshold) for the certificate of Algerian origin; whether the 23-12 implementing decree has been issued in 2024–2026.

### Inferences: GO / INVESTIGATE / KILL implications

**GO factors (policy supports the business)**
- Tariff escalation: 5% on presses, molds and resin vs 30% (+DAPS on some lines) on finished plastic articles.
- AAPI VAT franchise and duty exemption on the press, molds and auxiliaries.
- State pressure to localise. The GPI cutoff and OEM integration targets mean buyers *must* find local plastic suppliers in 2027.
- Banking friction (01/DGC/2026, Instr. 05-2026) and the PPI make importing finished parts slower and costlier for the *buyers' current suppliers*. That is a pain signal the molder can sell against: local lead time, no 120% provision, no 30-day lag.

**INVESTIGATE before committing**
1. GPI cahier des charges and part list: which automotive plastic parts are reserved for GPI, and does GPI sub-contract small parts? Contact ACS/GPI and Stellantis Oran purchasing.
2. DAPS and DD on the *exact* HS lines of the first 5–10 target products, especially when the import is classified as parts of machines/vehicles (ch. 84/85/87) rather than 3926. Use a transitaire or a DGD readout.
3. Bank: written confirmation from the domiciliating bank on how a 30% supplier deposit is handled under 01/DGC/2026, and how mold-design services are domiciled.
4. PPI calendar for H1 2027: file the company's first PPI covering press + molds + resin before the window closes (likely Dec 2026 – Feb 2027).
5. AAPI: confirm codes 1113xx (non-packaging) are not on the DE 22-300 annex. Confirm molds bought in later years can be added to the exempt list.
6. Sous-traitance exemption (2020 arrêté): is it still open? It could remove 5% duty + 19% VAT on resin for contracted OEM work.

**KILL triggers (watch, none currently active)**
- If the GPI cahier des charges reserves *all* automotive plastic parts (including small clips/fasteners) to GPI, drop automotive OEM as a beachhead and pivot to appliance, electrical and hydraulic components.
- If PLF 2027 cuts DD/DAPS on finished plastic articles, or extends exemptions to imported finished parts for assemblers. No sign of this.
- If the bank refuses any pre-shipment advance to Chinese mold makers, the "own molds from China" model would need L/C-based mold contracts. That is workable but slower; it becomes a KILL only if no bank will open L/Cs for a new SARL.
- A plan relying on a cheap 10–20-year-old European press is effectively **killed** by DE 24-241 (≤5 years).

### Gaps
- Official texts could not be read; every rate should be re-checked against the DGD tariff and JORA before purchase decisions. The research budget was exhausted before checking DAPS on 3917/3924/3925/3926.90, the content of the ABEF services measures, and 2026 Ministry of Industry sous-traitance statements.
