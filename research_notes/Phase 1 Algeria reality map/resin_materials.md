## 1. Who supplies resin, masterbatch and regrind in Algeria?

### Takeaway
There are enough named suppliers to start buying PP and HDPE in all three northern regions: **Polychimical in Skikda (East), Distripol in Zéralda (Algiers) and AB Polymers in Es Senia (Oran)**. A thin local masterbatch base also exists, with about five named makers. A recycled PP/HDPE granule base exists too, with **El Kader Plast in Mascara as the largest named**. No official Algerian distributor of SABIC, Borouge or any other brand was found, and no Algerian stockist of ABS, PC, POM, TPE or TPU was found.

### Inferences
- ESTIMATE (my arithmetic, not a quote). Distributor ex-VAT price for virgin PP or HDPE in Q3–Q4 2026 is likely **220–350 DZD/kg**:

- ESTIMATE: virgin-resin price therefore probably makes up **about 50–70% of the ex-works cost** of a simple PP/HDPE part. A ±30% resin swing can erase margin on commodity products.

## 3. Shortage history and 2026 supply risk

### Takeaway
Algeria has had **two distinct shortage shocks**. The **2020–21 shock was policy-driven**: import licensing halted supply, prices went from 170 to 520 DA/kg, and plants stopped for months. The **2026 shock is global**: the Strait of Hormuz closure after the February–March 2026 war cut Gulf polyolefin exports and raised PE/PP world prices by about 37–38%. Gulf origin (SABIC, Borouge) is the usual reference in Algeria, so the startup's supply chain is directly exposed.

### Cited Findings
- In 2021, prices rose more than 300% (170 to 520 DA/kg) and many plants stopped for months for lack of raw material. Importers then received licences, with imports expected within 30–45 days. — [Echorouk](https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%8A%D9%83-%D8%A8%D9%80300-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%86); recycled plastic rose from 100 to 250 DA/kg — [Maghreb Emergent](https://maghrebemergent.news/fr/hausse-des-prix-du-plastique-linevitable-obligation-de-recourir-a-limportation/)
- In 2026, Hormuz maritime traffic fell 97% and naphtha, ethylene and polyolefin exports were almost paralysed. PE was about 1,176 USD/t and PP about 1,288 USD/t. — [El Watan](https://elwatan.dz/flambee-des-prix-du-plastique-les-consommateurs-pris-en-etau/)
- PE rose 37% and PP 38% on world markets, with transits down more than 70%. — [El Djazair El Djadida](https://www.eldjazaireldjadida.dz/%D9%87%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B9%D9%83%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D8%B6%D8%B7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A5%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%8A%D9%83-%D8%A7/)
- European PP rose from about 1,300 to 2,500 EUR/t and PET by about 60%, to about 1,850 USD/t, between February and April 2026. Algerian transformers face "rising prices that signal an approaching raw-material supply crisis". — [Maghreb Emergent](https://maghrebemergent.news/fr/la-fermeture-dormuz-relance-les-projets-petrochimiques-algeriens/)
- In France, Elipso denounced "abusive force majeure" declarations by suppliers citing Hormuz. — [L'Usine Nouvelle](https://www.usinenouvelle.com/emballage/emballages-en-plastique-a-loffensive-elipso-denonce-des-forces-majeures-abusives-au-regard-des-principes-juridiques-internationaux-au-pretexte-du-blocage-du-detroit-dormuz.ICXZJN6HIBEEDHPOZQZR2SAUMU.html). A counter-signal says European output was running flat out and the risk of shortages stayed limited. — [L'Usine Nouvelle](https://www.usinenouvelle.com/polymeres-materiaux/materiaux/plastiques-et-composites/guerre-au-moyen-orient-avec-une-production-europeenne-des-plastiques-qui-tourne-a-fond-les-risques-de-ruptures-dans-la-plasturgie-devraient-rester-limites.DJXONOMFL5HSTOXNGT4THKTTTE.html)

## 4. What to stock first, and GO / INVESTIGATE / KILL

### Takeaway
Start with a **narrow, commodity material list**: one PP injection homopolymer, one PP impact copolymer, one HDPE injection grade, black and 2–3 colour masterbatches, and UV masterbatch for outdoor parts. Buy engineering resins only to order. The supply base supports a **GO on PP/HDPE**, but resin price is the main **INVESTIGATE** item.

### Cited Findings
- AB Polymers cites injection-grade HDPE and PP homo/copo, and Polychimical lists PP, PPR, PEHD and PEBD. — [Kompass](https://dz.kompass.com/a/resines-synthetiques/21860/); [adresse-algerie](https://adresse-algerie.com/fr/companies/sarl-polychimical)
- Local colour masterbatch makers exist in Tipaza, Blida, Annaba, Oran and Tizi Ouzou. — [Kompass](https://dz.kompass.com/a/pre-transformation-des-plastiques/65710/); [dzentreprise](https://dzentreprise.net/annuaire-professionnel/sarl-taif-master-batch/)
- Recycled PP/HDPE granules are offered locally (El Kader Plast, BRUM). — [Algérie Eco](https://algerie-eco.com/2022/09/13/recyclage-du-plastique-une-grande-usine-ouvrira-avant-fin-septembre-a-mascara/); [Kompass](https://dz.kompass.com/a/pre-transformation-des-plastiques/65710/)

### Inferences
- **Stock order (ASSUMPTION / engineering practice):**
  1. **PP homopolymer injection, MFR about 10–25** (575P-type). It covers housewares, caps and technical clips.
  2. **PP impact copolymer, MFR about 10–20**, for crates, buckets and parts that need low-temperature impact.
  3. **HDPE injection, MFR about 5–20**, for caps, pallet feet and spacers.
  4. **Black MB and white TiO2 MB**, then 2–3 customer colours.
  5. **UV/HALS MB** for any outdoor part.
  6. **In-house regrind** plus a qualified recycled PP/HDPE source for non-cosmetic parts.

  Hold **4–8 weeks of consumption** (about 2–5 t for one 110 t press on two shifts; ESTIMATE) as a 2026 shock buffer.
- **GO.** PP/HDPE and colour masterbatch are commercially available through at least three regional distributors and five masterbatch makers. Recycled PP/HDPE also exists. Supply is not a blocker for a single-press startup.
- **INVESTIGATE (before committing capex):**
  - (a) Call Polychimical, AB Polymers and Distripol for written quotes on 1 t of PP homo, PP copo and HDPE: grade, origin, DZD/kg ex-VAT, MOQ, lead time, price validity.
  - (b) Check Ouedkniss prices manually from an Algerian connection.
  - (c) Ask whether the distributors stock CP2K HDPE.
  - (d) Get masterbatch prices and MOQs from Himaplast, Taif and BRUM.
  - (e) Sample El Kader Plast and BRUM regrind: MFR, contamination, consistency.
  - (f) Build a cost model with resin at 220, 300 and 400 DZD/kg.
- **KILL triggers (product level, not project level):**
  - Any product idea that depends on PC, POM, TPU/TPE or ABS in small lots, unless a stockist is confirmed. None was found.
  - Any commodity product whose margin disappears at about 300+ DZD/kg resin.
  - Any plan that assumes local Arzew PP before 2028.

### Gaps
- A real DZD/kg price ladder (100 kg / 1 t / 5 t) from any Algerian distributor: NOT FOUND. This is the single most important missing input for Phase 2 economics.
