## 1. Cluster-by-cluster map

### Takeaway
Supply capability sits in three dense clusters: **East Algiers–Boumerdès** (Rouiba, Oued Smar, Kouba, Dar El Beida, Tassala El Merdja, Ouled Moussa, Khemis El Khechna, Bordj Menaiel), **Sétif–El Eulma** and **Bordj Bou Arréridj**. **Blida** is a secondary packaging cluster. **Oran** has only commodity molders on record. **Tissemsilt** is the new state auto pole (GPI). Constantine, Annaba, Skikda, Batna, Tizi Ouzou, Béjaïa and Sidi Bel Abbès show almost nothing beyond single names. Ghardaïa and Khenchela appeared unexpectedly.

- The West (Oran) appears to have commodity molders but no documented technical molder or mold maker. This is "thin capacity" on the evidence, though directories under-count heavily.

## 2. Dangerous competitors for small technical B2B parts

### Takeaway
The few firms able to match or copy a small technical-parts offer are mostly in the founder's likely area or in Sétif. **SNK Plastic** (Rouiba) and **MIRAF** (BBA) both do custom molding and molds. **MBS PLAST** (Sétif) covers medical precision. **ENPC/SIPLAST + ALMOULES**, **SAREL** and **GPI** cover automotive. Most other molders on record are in packaging or commodity goods.

### Cited Findings
- **SNK Plastic:** runs customer molds, 50–650 t, parts from 1 g to 2 kg, plus mold design, training and machine sales — [snkplastic.com](https://snkplastic.com/); [snk-plastic.com](https://snk-plastic.com/injection-plastique)
- **MIRAF:** makes high-precision plastic parts in small to large series, with custom mold development and traceable QC — [miraf.dz](https://miraf.dz/)
- **MBS PLAST:** medical injection and assembly in a clean room, with ISO 13485 and ISO 15378 — [Maghreb Pharma](https://www.maghrebpharma.com/en/exhibitors/mbs-plast-240861)
- **ENPC/SIPLAST:**
  - quotes a 6-week lead time on molds — [ENPC](https://www.enpc-dz.com/siplast/)
  - partners with Sigit on Stellantis parts — [Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/68308) (prior notes)
- **SAREL:** automotive plastics supplier to Stellantis — [Algérie Eco](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=78229)
- **GPI:** state plant, protected by the 2027 ban on imports of locally makeable auto plastic parts — [Algérie Eco](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/09/24/industrie-automobile-une-usine-de-production-de-composants-plastiques-inauguree-a-tissemsilt/)
- **Electrical boxes:** a local brand (HAOUAS) already sells junction boxes in retail, and a Boumerdès Ouedkniss ad offers "fabrication de boîte d'encastrement" (flush-mount box manufacturing) — [sogedim](https://sogedim.net/product/boite-etanche-electrique-80x80-mm/); Ouedkniss ad (prior notes)
- **Mold makers who could copy molds:** Zerouni, FMPI, Master Mill Precision, Precision Industry, CFM Engineering, Cumnewtec (Cumnewtec makes parts "from models, drawings").

### Inferences
- Threat ranking for a 100–120 t technical-parts shop near Algiers:
  1. **SNK Plastic:** same model, nearby, sees customers' molds.
  2. **Informal Ouedkniss molders and moulistes:** cheap, fast, imitative.
  3. **MIRAF:** strong in electrical and appliance parts for the BBA ecosystem.
  4. **Captive OEM shops:** they could in-source if a part proves profitable.
- MBS PLAST and SAREL/SIPLAST/GPI matter only if the founder targets medical or automotive parts.

### Gaps
- Press counts, brands and utilisation for SNK, MIRAF, MBS PLAST and Plastique 3000.
- Pricing behaviour of the main competitors.

## 3. Where capacity is crowded vs thin

### Takeaway
**Crowded:** preforms and caps, packaging (thermoforming, IML), household goods, pipes and fittings, large logistics parts. **Thin on record:** small technical, electrical and industrial parts made to customer drawings; mold repair services outside Algiers and Sétif; machine service for anything other than Yizumi.

### Cited Findings
- Preforms and caps:
  - SGT has 16 preform presses (Rouiba) plus cap sites in Sétif (2 bn caps/yr per prior notes) — [PETpla.net](https://petpla.net/assets/ebooks/PP201505/17/)
  - PTD is the #2 preform maker
  - BENAL (Khenchela) makes PET preforms
- Packaging: Smart Pack, Optimal Packaging Solutions, Thermoplast Atek, Etoile Plastique, and four BBA packaging firms (Kompass).
- Large logistics parts: Yizumi shipped its first 5,500 t machine, the largest it has ever exported, to an unnamed Algerian customer, citing demand for pallets and turnover boxes — [Yizumi](https://www.yizumi.com/en/news/product/IMM/yizumi-s-first-5500t-injection-molding-machine-heads-to-algeria)
- Formal listings are tiny against the sector's size:
  - Kompass lists only 12 injection molders and 9 mold companies nationally — [Kompass](https://dz.kompass.com/a/moulage-par-injection-des-plastiques-moulage-a-reaction-des/6577010/)
  - the sector has about 1,000 firms, about 40% doing injection — [Plasteurope](https://www.plasteurope.com/news/ALGERIA_t215050) (prior notes)

### Inferences
- New capital (AAPI's 675 plastics projects; Yizumi's large-tonnage shipments) flows mostly into commodity and packaging parts. A 100–120 t shop focused on technical B2B parts sits in a thinner segment, but faces informal copiers once a part becomes standard.

### Gaps
- Machine-hour capacity or utilisation by cluster. No sources were found.

## 5. Copying and imitation risk evidence

### Takeaway
There is general, mostly dated evidence that imitation is widespread and that much counterfeit production is local. No case was found specifically involving Algerian molders copying a customer's or competitor's part. The industry structure, with molders who also make molds, Ouedkniss moulistes and public reverse-engineering services, makes copying technically easy.

### Cited Findings
- In 2011 the customs DG said 60% of goods imported for resale were counterfeit — [Afrik.com](https://www.afrik.com/la-contrefacon-en-algerie)
- A Commerce Ministry survey found 40.98% of counterfeits were made locally, 41.34% imported and 18% of unknown origin. Auto spare parts are among the most counterfeited goods — [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/la-contrefacon-prend-de-lampleur-en-algerie-tout-sur-lindustrie-du-faux/); [Afrik.com](https://www.afrik.com/la-contrefacon-en-algerie)
- 700,000 articles were seized in 2011, up 845% — [Echorouk](https://www.echoroukonline.com/contrefacon-saisie-de-700-000-articles-une-hausse-de-845-en-2011)
- Press commentary on weak regulation:
  - "En l'absence d'une réglementation claire, la contrefaçon étouffe les marchés" ("without clear regulation, counterfeiting is choking markets") — [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/en-labsence-dune-reglementation-claire-la-contrefacon-etouffe-les-marches/)
  - informality and counterfeiting are eating away at the economy — [Algérie Eco 2018](https://algerie-eco.com/2018/11/18/linformel-et-la-contrefacon-gangrenent-leconomie/)
- Firms advertise copying-capable services: making parts from models or drawings (Cumnewtec), automotive spare parts (Precision Industry), reverse engineering (CDTA) — [cumnewtec](https://www.cumnewtec.com/contact/); [CDTA](https://www.cdta.dz/en/?p=12201)

### Inferences
- Copying risk is **highest** for the founder's own catalogue products (standard clips, boxes, plugs) and **lower** for parts made to a single customer's drawing under that customer's brand.
- The main channels are:
  - giving parts or molds to a molder who also makes molds, for overflow work;
  - mold makers reusing designs;
  - employees moving between nearby firms.

### Gaps
- No forum, social-media or court evidence of molder-to-molder copying was found. Interviews are needed.
- Counterfeit data is 2011–2013 and not specific to plastics.

## 6. GO / INVESTIGATE / KILL implications

### Takeaway
On supply-side evidence the plan is **GO-conditional** for small technical B2B parts. The supporting ecosystem exists: dealers with local service, mold makers, setters. The technical segment looks thinner than packaging or commodity goods. **INVESTIGATE** competitor depth and copying behaviour by field interview. **KILL** any packaging, caps, household or large-logistics-parts angle.

### Cited Findings
- Crowded segments, scale players and the technical-parts competitors are as documented in sections 2–3.
- Dealer and service routes are as documented in section 4.

### Inferences
- **GO (conditional):**
  - Buy through a dealer with local service (AFC/Yizumi, or Plasticolor/Chen Hsong as a competing quote).
  - Keep molds in-house.
  - Target drawing-specific B2B parts: electrical, appliance-spares and machine-builder parts.
- **INVESTIGATE:**
  - (a) SNK's and MIRAF's real press counts, utilisation and prices;
  - (b) the informal Ouedkniss molder density around the chosen site;
  - (c) which freelance technicians service Haitian and Chinese presses;
  - (d) a mold maker's NDA and design-reuse practice (Zerouni, FMPI, Master Mill);
  - (e) who bought Yizumi's 5,500 t machine;
  - (f) Condor's real status.
- **KILL:** preforms and caps (SGT, PTD), thin-wall/IML packaging (Thermoplast Atek, Yizumi PAC buyers), household chairs and buckets, and medical parts without ISO 13485 (MBS PLAST).

### Gaps
- All of the above needs phone or field validation. Pages were blocked and the search budget was exhausted this session.
